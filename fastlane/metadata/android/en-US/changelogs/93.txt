First store build of OpenConnect 1.13.0. The app is the same as the GitHub build, without the in-app updater: no install-packages permission and no FileProvider. It targets Android 16 (API 36) and still supports Android 9 and newer. Your data stays on the phone; no OpenConnect account or server is needed. Garmin, Oura, AI and Health Connect remain optional, and the store build does not contact GitHub for updates.
