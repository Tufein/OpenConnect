## OpenConnect v0.26.0: your training plan on the watch

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.26.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.25.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.26.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Planned sessions become real workouts.** Under *Insights, Plan* there is a new card, *Workouts on your watch*. Every
  session of the next 7 days is written as a structured Garmin workout in the FIT format:
  - easy run, long run and race: one step with the distance;
  - tempo: 10 minutes warm-up, the tempo distance (session length minus 2.5 km) at a pace window, 5 minutes cool-down;
  - intervals: warm-up, 4 to 10 repeats of 400 m fast plus 90 s jog (repeats from the session length), cool-down.
- **Targets use your own paces.** They come from the predicted 10 km pace of v0.25.0: tempo about 3 percent slower, intervals
  about 4 percent faster, with a window of 8 seconds per kilometre either side. Without a prediction (no run of 4 km or more)
  the steps have no pace target.
- **Copied over the USB cable** into `GARMIN/NewFiles`, the same way as the watch faces. A file that is already on the
  watch is left alone, so pressing the button twice does no harm. File names look like `OC_2026-11-16_TEMPO.fit`.
- The Settings button for the Connect IQ store now opens the real listing once there is a store id, and Garmin's store home
  until then.

### Not verified

- **No fenix 8 has seen these files yet.** They are checked with an independent FIT reader (header, CRC, field values) but
  only your watch can say whether it lists them under *Training, Workouts*. If it does not, tell me what it says.
- Copying needs the watch in file-transfer (MTP) mode, as for the watch faces. Sharing as a file is not built; USB only.

### What changed in the code

- `core/Workouts.kt` (new): the workout model (`Workout`, `WorkoutBlock`, `WorkoutStep`, `StepLength`), `TrainingPaces`,
  `Workouts.forSession` (session to steps), `fitSteps` (repeat steps as FIT wants them), `toFit`, `fileName`,
  `upcoming` (the next 7 days, never before the plan starts) and `send`.
- `core/formats/Fit.kt`: the FIT writer learned string fields and the workout (`file_id` type 5, `workout`, `workout_step`)
  messages; `exportWorkout` builds the file and `finish` adds header and CRC, shared with the activity export.
- `core/watch`: `WatchSource.installWorkout`; the MTP source writes into `NewFiles`, the in-memory one is used by tests.
- `core/WorkoutsTest.kt`: reads every file back with `FitReader` and checks the steps, repeat pointers, pace windows,
  names, the 7-day window and the transfer including the "already there" case.
- App: `AppViewModel.sendPlanToWatch`, the card in `PlanScreen.kt`, strings in English and Dutch; `WorkoutsScreenTest`
  creates a plan, tries without a watch, then with a fake USB watch, then again.
- Python version, `versionName` and `versionCode` moved to 0.26.0; the plan UI tests scroll to the plan buttons because the
  race card now sits above them.

### Samenvatting (Nederlands)

Op het tabblad Plan staat nu *Trainingen op je horloge*: elke sessie van de komende 7 dagen wordt een gestructureerde
Garmin-training (warming-up, intervallen of tempo, cooldown) met tempodoelen uit je voorspelde 10 km-tempo. De app zet de
bestanden via de USB-kabel in `GARMIN/NewFiles`. Of je fenix 8 ze accepteert is alleen met het horloge zelf te zien; de
bestanden zijn wel met een onafhankelijke FIT-lezer gecontroleerd.
