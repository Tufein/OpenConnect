## OpenConnect v0.24.0: the road to the Connect IQ store

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.24.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.23.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.24.0.apk.sha256`.

### The question: can the app link straight to the store?

**Yes, once the apps are published, not before.** An app's store address is `https://apps.garmin.com/apps/<store id>`,
and Garmin issues the store id at the first upload (it is not the id inside the manifest), so it cannot be known in
advance. The app is ready: *Settings, Watch face preview, Connect IQ store* shows a button per app as soon as you paste
the store id (or the whole store address) into `ConnectIqStore.kt`; until then it says the apps are not in the store yet.

### What is new

- **Settings in Garmin's Connect IQ app.** Design, accent colour and the four fields of the face (and colour and fields of
  the widget and glance) become real settings, in English and Dutch. Anything missing or invalid falls back to what the
  app was built with, so the face is never blank.
- **A store package:** `watch/build.sh --package --type watchface` (or `widget`) packs one `.iq` with every fenix 8
  model and a fixed app id per app.
- **`watch/check-store.sh`** checks what can be checked without Garmin's login, and validates the resource files against
  Garmin's own schema when the SDK is there. The test suite runs it.
- **Store texts** in English and Dutch, a screenshot list, and **[docs/CONNECT_IQ_STORE.md](docs/CONNECT_IQ_STORE.md)**
  with every step you have to take yourself (Garmin login, SDK devices, developer key, upload, review, store id).
- **A new roadmap in Dutch** for v0.24.0 to v0.29.0 ([docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md)).

### Not verified

- The Monkey C, including the new settings code, is **still not compiled** (the device files need your Garmin login).
- The `.iq` has not been built or uploaded, so Garmin has not validated it. The settings files do validate against
  `resources.xsd` from the Connect IQ 9.3.0 SDK.
- The store link in the app is switched off until you give it a store id.

### What changed in the code

- `watch/source/Prefs.mc` (new): reads `Application.Properties` (`Design`, `Colour`, `Field1` to `Field4`) and falls back
  to `Config`; `Faces.mc`, `FaceView.mc` and `OpenConnectGlance.mc` ask `Prefs` now; both apps refresh on
  `onSettingsChanged`.
- `watch/build.sh`: `--package`, `--products`, the version read from `pyproject.toml`, and generated
  `properties.xml` and `settings.xml`; `watch/manifest.xml` lists the products and the Dutch language;
  `watch/monkey.jungle` adds `resources-nld`; the setting names are in `resources/strings` and `resources-nld/strings`.
- `watch/check-store.sh` (new) and `tests/test_watch_store.py`: package names, products, the fixed app ids, defaults
  against the choices, English and Dutch strings, the listing texts, and a failing run when versions differ.
- Android: `core/watch/ConnectIqStore.kt` (address building from a store id or a pasted address, anything else refused),
  its test, the Settings card and strings (English and Dutch).

### Samenvatting (Nederlands)

De link naar de store kan alleen naar de app wijzen nadat jij de app hebt gepubliceerd: Garmin geeft het store-id pas
bij de eerste upload. Alles wat vooraf kon staat klaar: instellingen in Garmins app, een `.iq`-build, een controlescript,
store-teksten in het Nederlands en Engels en een lege constante in de app die je na de upload vult. De stappen voor jou
staan in `docs/CONNECT_IQ_STORE.md`. De Monkey C is nog niet gecompileerd.
