## OpenConnect v0.17.0: choose your devices, Oura on its own tab

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.17.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.16.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.17.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **The first start asks which devices you use.** Garmin, Oura Ring, both or neither. Only what you choose shows up:
  - Without Garmin, Home has no sync status, no "connect your Garmin" card and no Garmin or watch buttons.
  - Without a ring, there is no Oura tab and the app never contacts Oura.
  - You can change this any time under *Settings, Your devices*. Switching a device off keeps its data and its login.
  - People updating from v0.16.0 see this question once as well.
- **Oura Ring on its own tab.** The ring's data is no longer mixed into the watch's days, nights and activities. The new
  **Oura** tab shows:
  - the latest day's readiness, sleep and activity scores (tap one to jump to its chart);
  - last night: time asleep, deep and REM sleep, lowest heart rate, HRV and skin temperature;
  - 30-day charts for readiness, sleep, steps, lowest heart rate, HRV and temperature, readable by touch;
  - the ring's last ten workouts.

  The login and the sync settings sit below the charts.
- **Six tabs still fit.** With the Oura tab, the bar uses shorter words: *Sport*, *Gezond* and *Opties* in Dutch.
- **Demo data** includes 30 ring days when the Oura Ring is switched on, so you can look around the tab first.

### Coming from v0.16.0

- The update moves the ring to its own storage, and the ring's workouts leave the activity list.
- The ring's numbers that v0.16.0 filled into empty fields of the watch's days cannot be told apart, so they stay.
- The switch *Use the ring's sleep instead of the watch's* is gone, because the two are now kept apart.
- Sync the ring once (*Oura tab, Sync now*) to fill its tab with the last 30 days.

### What changed in the code

- `core/oura/OuraData.kt` (new): `OuraDay` (one day with Oura's scores, night, heart rate, HRV, SpO2, temperature and
  workouts, stored as JSON) and `OuraWorkout`; `OuraDemo` makes the demo days.
- `core/oura/OuraSync.kt`:
  - It now also reads `daily_readiness`, and writes only `OuraDay` rows.
  - It no longer touches `DailySummary`, `SleepSummary` or `Activity`.
  - A workout without Oura's `day` falls back to its start date.
- `Repository`: `upsertOuraDay` and `listOuraDays`. SQLite moves to schema version 3 with a new `oura_days` table; the
  upgrade removes v0.16.0's ring workouts from `activities`. `clearAll` and `Backup` include the ring's days.
- `SettingsStore`: `onboarded`, `useGarmin` (default on) and `useOura` (default off). `AppViewModel.sources` and
  `setSources` save them.
  - The automatic syncs and the Oura background job only run for a device that is switched on.
  - `AppContainer.applySources` starts or stops the Oura job.
- `OuraController`: the stored days are part of `OuraUi` (`refreshDays`), and `preferSleep` is gone.
- UI:
  - `OnboardingScreen` is new, and `DeviceSwitch` is a new shared component.
  - Navigation shows the onboarding until it is answered and adds the Oura tab (with a ring icon) only when chosen.
    `Tab.compact` holds the short labels.
  - `OuraScreen` is now a tab with the dashboard on top.
  - `HomeScreen` takes `useGarmin` and `onOura`.
  - Settings has the new *Your devices* card.
- Tests:
  - `OuraTest` covers the separate storage, the scores and that the watch's data stays untouched. `BackupTest` covers
    the ring's days.
  - `OuraAndTouchScreenTest` covers the first-start choice, that there is no Oura tab or Oura call without a ring, the
    Dutch Oura tab with demo days, and that every label fits its tab with six tabs.
  - `AppTestBase.launchFresh` marks the first start as answered unless a test asks otherwise.

### Not verified

- The background job and the onboarding on a real phone.
