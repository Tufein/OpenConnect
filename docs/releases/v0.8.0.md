## OpenConnect v0.8.0: scores, and a sportier look

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.8.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.7.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.8.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Scores on Health.** A new *Scores* section opens the screen with four tiles: **Readiness** (OpenConnect's own 0 to 100
  estimate), **Sleep score** (the number your watch gives each night), **Body Battery** and **Stress**. Each tile shows
  the latest value and a segmented bar; a dash means no reading yet. Under them come two 30-day charts, *Readiness* and
  *Sleep score*, with the same plain-language caption as the other charts (verdict, average, best and worst day, trend,
  what the number means).
- **A sportier, more unique look.** The cards have two cut corners instead of round ones, headlines lean forward in heavy
  italics like a race bib, section titles start with three speed stripes in the app's colours, and every score has a
  bar of 20 slanted segments that fills up like a HUD. Labels and small numbers stay in the monospace scoreboard font.
  Colours per signal and the dark and light themes are unchanged.

### What changed in the code

- `StatsService.readinessSeries()` computes the readiness for every day against the 28 days before it, with the same
  calculation as the ring on Home; `sleepScoreSeries()` gives the watch's score per night. 4 tests.
- `ChartMetric` gained `READINESS` and `SLEEP_SCORE` (good from 70 and 80, fair from 40 and 60). 1 new test.
- `Theme.kt`: `CutCornerShape` card shapes and a heavy-italic type scale. `Components.kt`: `SpeedStripes`, `ScoreTile`
  and `SegmentBar`; `SectionTitle` uses the stripes. `HealthScreen.kt`: the Scores section and the two charts.
- Version 0.8.0, versionCode 8. English and Dutch strings.

### Known limits

- Garmin's own Training Readiness, Training Status and VO2 max are not scores the Personal API hands over, so they are
  not shown. Readiness is OpenConnect's estimate, not Garmin's.
- The Bluetooth sync is still untested on a real watch.
