## OpenConnect v0.18.0: themes and watch faces

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.18.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.17.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.18.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Seven colour themes.** *Settings, Appearance, Colours*. Each one works in dark and light, and with *Follow phone*.
  - **Arcade:** the original mint on indigo.
  - **Game Boy:** the four greens of the 1989 handheld, the signal colours included.
  - **Amber terminal:** amber phosphor on black.
  - **Synthwave:** neon pink and cyan on deep purple.
  - **Ocean** and **Sunset.**
  - **Wallpaper:** your phone's own colours, on Android 12 and newer.
- **Three styles.** *Settings, Appearance, Style*.
  - **Sport:** cut corners and leaning headlines, as before.
  - **Round:** soft corners and upright text.
  - **Pixel:** square corners and everything in monospace, like an old terminal.
- **Signal colours stay recognisable.** Heart rate, sleep, stress and Body Battery keep their colour in every theme
  except Game Boy. Steps and readiness follow the theme's main colour.
- **Watch face over USB.** *Settings, Watch face*: connect the Garmin with its USB cable and pick a `.prg` file. It is
  copied to `GARMIN/Apps` on the watch, the usual sideload folder. You then choose it on the watch (hold the menu
  button, *Watch Face*). A button opens the Connect IQ store.

### What is honest about watch faces

- **The phone cannot switch the active watch face.** Garmin offers no open way to do that, and the Bluetooth sync this
  app uses (ported from Gadgetbridge) has no such command.
- **Faces from Garmin's store go through Garmin's own Connect IQ app**, because they are tied to your watch.
- **The USB button is for a `.prg` you built yourself** with the Connect IQ SDK, or got from its maker. It has not been
  tried on a real watch yet: the MTP write is tested against a fake watch only.

### What changed in the code

- `ui/Theme.kt`:
  - `Palette` and `LookStyle` enums.
  - `scheme()` builds a full Material colour scheme from six key colours per theme; dynamic colours are used on
    Android 12+.
  - Three typographies (`SportTypography`, `RoundTypography`, `PixelTypography`) and three shape sets.
  - `metricsFor()` decides the signal colours per theme.
  - `OpenConnectTheme(mode, palette, style)`.
- `SettingsStore.palette` and `lookStyle`. `AppViewModel.look` and `setLook`. `MainActivity` passes them to the theme.
- Settings, Appearance: chips with a colour dot per theme and the style chips. A new Watch face card.
- `core/watch/WatchFace.kt` (new): `WatchFace.check`/`install`, a safe file name, and `WatchFaceException` with the reasons.
- Watch side:
  - `WatchSource.installApp` and `MtpTransport.createFolder`/`upload`.
  - `MtpWatchSource` finds or makes `GARMIN/Apps` and never overwrites a file of the same name.
  - `UsbWatchLink` writes with `MtpDevice.sendObjectInfo` plus `sendObject`.
- Tests:
  - `WatchFaceTest` (core) covers the Apps folder, a missing folder, refused files, a second copy and a read-only link.
  - `ThemesAndWatchFaceTest` (app) checks that a theme and style are picked and kept, with screenshots, and copies a
    face to a fake watch on the cable.

### Not verified

The MTP write to a real Garmin, and the Wallpaper theme on a real phone.
