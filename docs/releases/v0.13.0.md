## OpenConnect v0.13.0: Health Connect

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.13.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.12.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.13.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

*Settings, Health Connect.* Nothing happens until you tap *Allow access in Health Connect* and say yes on Android's own screen.

- **Send activities.** *Send activities now* writes every activity (type, start, end, distance and calories when known) to
  Health Connect, so Google Fit, Samsung Health and other apps can show them. Each activity has a fixed id
  (`openconnect-<id>`), so sending again replaces instead of duplicating. A switch can do it after every sync.
- **Read steps and sleep.** *Read steps and sleep* looks at the last 30 days and fills only what OpenConnect has no
  value for: a day without steps, a night without sleep. What your watch gave is never overwritten. Activities are not read
  back, so the same run cannot exist twice.

### What changed in the code

- `core/health/HealthConnectSync.kt`: the rules (`export`, `import`, batches of 100, activities with no duration skipped and
  counted, nothing that came from Health Connect is sent back), with a `HealthConnectGateway` interface so they are tested
  without a phone (`HealthConnectSyncTest`).
- `health/AndroidHealthConnectGateway.kt`: the real Health Connect, with the permission list, the exercise-type mapping and
  paging for sleep. `health/HealthConnectController.kt`: state for the card and the automatic send.
- Manifest: the five health permissions, the Health Connect package query, the "how this app uses your data" entries.
- Settings card with three states (not available, needs permission, ready), tested with a fake gateway
  (`HealthConnectScreenTest`, `HealthConnectControllerTest`).
- Library: `androidx.health.connect:connect-client:1.1.0-alpha10`. The newer 1.1.0 needs compileSdk 36 and a newer Android
  Gradle plugin than this project uses; moving up is a separate step.

### Honest limits

- **Not tried against a real Health Connect.** The rules and the screen are tested; the calls into Health Connect compile but
  have only been checked against the library's API, not on a phone. The first real attempt may need a fix, and the message on
  the card shows Health Connect's own error text.
- Health Connect is built into Android 14 and newer; on Android 8 to 13 it is an app from Google Play.
- Step totals from Health Connect include whatever other apps wrote there, which may differ from your watch.
- The alpha library can change; it is pinned.
