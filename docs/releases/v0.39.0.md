## OpenConnect v0.39.0: a hi-score card for your year, and a season plan to your race

Last release from the app comparison in [APP-VERGELIJKING.md](../APP-VERGELIJKING.md) (the hi-score card and the season plan from the "later" list).

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.39.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.38.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.39.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Hi-score** (*Insights, Hi-score*; from the Strava year in sport and the Runalyze poster). One card per year, in the arcade look:
  activities, distance (and how much more or less than the year before), time, active days, the longest streak of days, the longest
  run and ride with their dates, the biggest climb, the busiest month, steps and your best step day. Pick another year with the chips.
  **Share as text** sends one line with the main numbers through Android's share sheet. Nothing leaves the phone before you press it.
- **Season plan** (*Insights, Plan*, under the race goal; from Intervals.icu's phases). With a race goal set, the weeks until the race
  are cut into **base, build, peak and taper**, counted back from the race: taper 1 week for 5K and 10K, 2 for a half marathon, 3 for a
  marathon; peak 2, 3 and 3 weeks; build 3, 4 and 4 weeks; everything before that is base. Each phase shows its dates, a range of
  kilometres a week around what you run now, and one line on what it is for. The phase you are in is marked. Because the phases are counted
  from the race, a phase starts in the same week however often you open the app. With too few weeks the front phases fall away
  (three weeks to a half marathon: one week peak and two weeks taper). A race more than a year away plans the last 52 weeks.

### Not verified

- Nothing ran on a phone; the share sheet was not opened (the screens are drawn and tested on the JVM, the intent is the plain
  `ACTION_SEND` text one).
- The phase lengths and the percentages of weekly kilometres (base 95 to 105, build 105 to 120, peak 120 to 130, taper 50 to 70) are
  rules of thumb, pinned by tests so they can be tuned. This is not a training plan from a coach, and not medical advice.

### What changed in the code

- `core/SeasonPlan.kt` (new): `SeasonPlanner.plan` (blocks built from the back, `current`), `weeklyKm`, `SeasonPhase`, `SeasonBlock`, `SeasonPlan`.
- `core/YearScore.kt` (new): `YearScoreCalculator.compute` and `years`, `YearScore` (the records reuse `RecordEntry`).
- `PlanState.season` is filled in `loadPlan` and after a race goal change; `ScoreState` and `loadScore` in `AppViewModel`; `ui/SeasonCard.kt`
  and `ui/ScoreScreen.kt` (new); `InsightsTab.Score`. Strings in English and Dutch.
- Tests: `SeasonPlanTest` (10) and `YearScoreTest` (9) in `core`, `ScoreAndSeasonScreenTest` (2) for the screens.

### What was not built from the comparison

Power analysis (FTP, normalised power, TSS), a watch companion with notifications and weather, upload to Intervals.icu, segments and
social features, lab results and biological age. They need a power meter, a store listing or someone else's service, or are not a fit for an
app that keeps your data on the phone. See [APP-VERGELIJKING.md](../APP-VERGELIJKING.md).
