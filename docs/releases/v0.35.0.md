## OpenConnect v0.35.0: shape for each distance, and VO2max from your runs

Second release from the app comparison in [APP-VERGELIJKING.md](../APP-VERGELIJKING.md) (ideas 3 and 7).

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.35.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.34.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.35.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

Both are on *Insights, Plan*, under your plan (or the plan setup).

- **Shape for each distance** (from Runalyze's *Marathon Shape*). Your last six weeks of running against what a typical plan asks
  for 5 km, 10 km, a half marathon and a marathon: average kilometres a week and your longest run. Each distance gets a bar,
  a percentage and a plain word (*Ready*, *Almost there*, *Building*, *Far off*), and the distance of your race goal (or the
  marathon) gets the numbers spelled out. It makes the race predictions honest: a marathon time worked out from a 5 km, when
  you run 15 km a week, is a long shot, and now the app says so.
- **VO2max from your runs** (from Runalyze's *effective VO2max*). Every run of 15 minutes and 2.5 km or more with a heart rate gives
  an estimate from its pace and its heart rate. You see the middle value of your last runs of the last eight weeks, a chart of the
  runs, and **the race times that VO2max stands for** next to the existing Riegel predictions.

### Not verified

- Nothing ran on a phone; the cards are drawn and tested on the JVM.
- The weekly kilometres and longest runs a distance asks (5 km: 20 and 8, 10 km: 30 and 12, half: 40 and 18, marathon: 55 and 30)
  and the verdict limits (90, 70 and 40 percent) are rules of thumb, pinned by tests so they can be tuned.
- The VO2max is an estimate, a few ml either way: heat, hills and tired legs are not counted, and it is not Garmin's VO2 max. The
  race times follow the published tables (a VO2max of 50 gives 19:57 for 5 km and 3:10:49 for a marathon, within one percent).

### What changed in the code

- `core/RaceShape.kt` (new): `RaceShapeCalculator.compute` (six-week window, weekly kilometres and the longest run as shares of
  the requirement, capped at 1) and `RaceShape.verdict`.
- `core/Vo2Max.kt` (new): oxygen cost of running (Daniels and Gilbert), the share of VO2max you can hold for a time, `fromRaceEffort`,
  `predictSeconds` (bisection), `estimateFromRun` (Swain: heart rate share = 0.64 * VO2max share + 0.37) and `summary`.
- `PlanState` has `shapes` and `vo2`, filled in `loadPlan`; `ui/ShapeVo2Cards.kt` (new) draws them, `PlanScreen` puts them after the
  plan so the plan buttons stay on top. Strings in English and Dutch.
- Tests: `RaceShapeTest` (5), `Vo2MaxTest` (10) in `core`, `RaceShapeScreenTest` for the cards.

### Samenvatting (Nederlands)

Onder *Inzichten, Plan* staan nu twee nieuwe kaarten. **Conditie per afstand**: je laatste zes weken hardlopen (kilometers per week
en je langste loop) tegenover wat een gewoon schema vraagt voor 5 km, 10 km, halve en hele marathon, met een balk, een percentage en
een duidelijk woord (*Klaar*, *Bijna*, *Aan het opbouwen*, *Nog ver weg*). Daarmee is een voorspelling voor een afstand waar je ver
van af zit niet meer misleidend. **VO2max uit je loopjes**: elk loopje van minstens 15 minuten en 2,5 km met hartslag geeft een
schatting uit tempo en hartslag; je ziet de middelste waarde, een grafiek en de **racetijden bij die VO2max**. Het zijn schattingen
(een paar ml erbij of eraf), niet Garmins VO2 max, en nog niet op een echte telefoon bekeken.
