## OpenConnect v0.38.0: a heatmap of your own routes, and the same route compared

Fifth release from the app comparison in [APP-VERGELIJKING.md](../APP-VERGELIJKING.md) (idea 8, and the matched activities from the "later" list).

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.38.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.37.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.38.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Map** (*Insights, Map*; from Strava's personal heatmap). All your routes on one plain grid. A square counts once for every
  activity that went through it, however long you stood there, so the road you run every week is brighter than the place you stopped for
  a photo. Four brightness steps on a log scale. There is no background map: the picture is only your own routes, so no map provider
  ever sees where you go. It reads the newest 300 activities and is worked out when you open the tab.
- **Same route** (on an activity; from Garmin's and Strava's matched activities). When you have run, cycled or walked the same route
  before, the activity shows how many times, its place by time (*Number 2 of 5*), how far behind your best it was and when that best
  was, and how it compares with your average on that route. Two activities are the same route when they are the same sport, within 10
  percent of each other's distance, and start, end and the middle of the track are within 300 metres. Times are compared at the distance
  of the activity you look at.

### Not verified

- Nothing ran on a phone; the screens are drawn and tested on the JVM, with tracks made up by the tests.
- The limits (10 percent, 300 metres, a grid of 48 squares, 300 activities) are rules of thumb. A route run in the other direction
  counts as another route (start and end are swapped), unless it is a loop.
- A very large history makes the heatmap take a moment, because every track has to be read from the database once.

### What changed in the code

- `core/Heatmap.kt` (new): `Heatmap.build` (flat x/y with longitude scaled by the cosine of the latitude, bounds with 1 percent cut off at
  each end when there are 200 positions or more, each route counts once per square, sparse tracks are joined in steps of half a square),
  `HeatGrid.level` (log scale, 0 to 4).
- `core/RouteMatcher.kt` (new): `isSameRoute`, `matches`, `history` and `RouteHistory`.
- `AppViewModel` has `MapState` and `loadMap` (newest 300 activities with a distance, each thinned to at most 500 positions) and fills
  `DetailState.route` when an activity is opened (only the activities of the same sport and a close distance have their track read). The
  Map tab loads only while it is open, and is marked stale after a refresh. `ui/MapScreen.kt` (new) has `HeatCanvas`, `MapContent` and
  `RouteHistoryCard`. Strings in English and Dutch.
- Tests: `HeatmapTest` (7) and `RouteMatcherTest` (7) in `core`, `MapScreenTest` (3) for the screens.
