## OpenConnect v0.41.0: a training ramp card, this week against your usual

First release of the "many small releases before midnight" run, with one small, well-tested idea each (from the "later" list in
[APP-VERGELIJKING.md](../APP-VERGELIJKING.md): the acute to chronic workload idea of TrainingPeaks and Intervals.icu).

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.41.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.40.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.41.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Training ramp** (*Insights, Form*, above the monotony card). Your last 7 days of training against the average week of the four
  weeks before. Four verdicts: *Lighter than usual* (under 0.8 times), *Right on your usual* (0.8 to 1.3), *More than usual* (1.3
  to 1.5) and *A big jump* (1.5 and up, the pattern that goes with overuse injuries). The card shows the load of the week, your usual
  week and the factor. It needs five weeks of history; with less, or when the four weeks before were empty, it stays away.

### Not verified

- Nothing ran on a phone; the card is drawn and tested on the JVM with demo data.
- The thresholds (0.8, 1.3, 1.5) are the usual rule of thumb from sports science, not something proven for you. It is an estimate in
  OpenConnect's own load units, not medical advice.

### What changed in the code

- `core/RampRate.kt` (new): `RampRateCalculator.compute(activities, today)` sums the load of each UTC day with
  `TrainingLoadCalculator.activityLoad` (the same load the Form tab uses), takes the last 7 days and the four 7-day windows before
  them, and returns a `RampRate` (ratio, week load, usual week load, `RampLevel` LOW, STEADY, HIGH or SPIKE). The edges belong to the
  higher level. Activities after today are ignored.
- `AppViewModel` fills `HomeState.ramp` in `loadHome`; `Navigation` hands it to `InsightsScreen`, which passes it to `FormContent`.
  `RampCard` (new, in `ui/RecoveryCards.kt`) draws it in the Handheld style. Strings in English and Dutch.
- Tests: `RampRateTest` (7) in `core`, `RampScreenTest` (1) for the card.
