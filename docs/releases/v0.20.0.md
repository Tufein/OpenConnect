## OpenConnect v0.20.0: watch face preview

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.20.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.19.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.20.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **A round preview of your watch face.** *Settings, Watch face preview*. It shows the face as it will look on a
  round 454 px watch (the fenix 8 AMOLED), with your own numbers of today.
  - **Face:** Clean (a ring around the big time), Dashboard (four tiles on black) or Retro (pixel font and a segment
    bar).
  - **Colour:** Mint, Blue, Amber, Pink or Green.
  - **Fields:** Body Battery, Heart rate, Stress, Sleep and Steps. You show four of them. The first one is the big one:
    tap a chosen field to make it the big one, tap another to replace the last.
- **The choices are kept** and are what the build script for the real face will use (v0.21.0).

### What this is not

It is a drawing in the app. Nothing is sent to the watch, and the preview does not change the face that is on it. The
real faces are built from Garmin's Connect IQ tools in the next versions.

### What changed in the code

- `ui/WatchPreview.kt` (new):
  - `WatchFacePreview` draws the three faces with Compose on a round 454 px canvas, scaled to the size you give it.
  - `WatchField`, `WatchColor`, `WatchFaceConfig` and `WatchFaceData` hold the choices and the numbers.
  - `WatchFaceConfig.buildCommand()` gives the `watch/build.sh` command for the choices.
- `SettingsStore`: `watchDesign`, `watchColor` and `watchFields`. `AppViewModel.watchFace` and `setWatchFace` keep them.
- `Navigation` builds the numbers from the same data as Home (`watchFaceData`). Settings has the new card.
- Tests: `WatchPreviewTest` covers the choices, that they are kept, the build command and the fallback to the default
  fields, with screenshots.

### Not verified

The preview on a real phone, and how close it is to a real watch (it is a drawing, not a Garmin simulator).
