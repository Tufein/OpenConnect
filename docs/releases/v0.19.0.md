## OpenConnect v0.19.0: three designs

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.19.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.18.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.19.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Three designs.** *Settings, Appearance, Design*. Each one changes how Home is laid out, how cards and charts are
  drawn and how the tab bar shows the open tab.
  - **Clean Pro:** calm cards without borders. The readiness ring sits on top, then a "My day" list with steps and one
    row per signal. Line charts get a soft coloured area underneath, and bars have rounded tops.
  - **Dashboard Dark:** the four signal tiles first, in two columns, on pure black. Bars are rounded and today's bar is
    lit. The tab bar shows the open tab only by its colour.
  - **Retro Pro:** the arcade look of earlier versions, with fine borders, glows and chart lines that glow like neon.
- **A design brings its own colours and style.** Clean Pro uses the new *Clean* colours (blue on grey and white) and
  Dashboard Dark the new *Midnight* colours (green on black). Both use the Round style. You can still pick any colour
  theme and style afterwards.
- **Charts and rings draw themselves in.** Lines draw from left to right, bars grow up and rings fill when they appear.
- **New installs start in Clean Pro**, and the first-start screen lets you pick a design. An update from v0.18.0 keeps
  Retro Pro, so nothing changes until you pick another one.

### What changed in the code

- `ui/Theme.kt`:
  - New `Design` enum (`CLEAN`, `DASHBOARD`, `RETRO`), each with its own palette and style.
  - `LocalDesign` passes the design down to every screen.
  - `cardBorder()` and `glowsCards()` keep borders and glows for Retro only.
  - Two new palettes, `CLEAN` and `MIDNIGHT`.
  - `OpenConnectTheme` takes a `design` argument.
- `SettingsStore.design` (key `theme_design`, default `retro`), plus `hasDesign`. `AppViewModel.setDesign` stores the design
  together with its palette and style. A new install (not yet onboarded, no design stored) starts in Clean.
- `HomeScreen`:
  - The cards are now small building blocks: `readiness()`, `goalsAndLoad()`, `stepsCard()` and the four signal tiles.
  - Each design orders them its own way, and Clean shows the signals as a full-width list under "My day".
- `Charts.kt`:
  - `rememberReveal` animates a chart from 0 to 1 in 0.7 s.
  - `LineChart` adds the area (Clean) or the glow (Retro).
  - `BarChart` rounds the bars and lights today (Dashboard).
- `Components.kt`: `RingGauge` fills up with an animation, and the cards use `cardBorder()`/`glowsCards()`.
- `Navigation`: the tab bar's indicator follows the design.
- `Settings` and `OnboardingScreen`: the shared `DesignChoice` chips with a line about each design.
- Tests:
  - `DesignsTest` covers picking each design, that colours and style come along and are kept, the Home layout per
    design, light mode, and the choice at the first start. It also takes screenshots.
  - `PlanAndLockScreenTest` now taps the lock switch through its semantic action, because the longer Settings screen
    can put it behind the tab bar.
