## OpenConnect v0.36.0: a journal for your days, and a heads-up when your body is off

Third release from the app comparison in [APP-VERGELIJKING.md](../APP-VERGELIJKING.md) (ideas 2 and 4).

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.36.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.35.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.36.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Journal** (from WHOOP's Journal and Oura's Tags). A card at the bottom of *Home* lets you mark today or yesterday with a few
  things: *Alcohol*, *Late meal*, *Late caffeine*, *Stressful day*, *Travel*, *Feeling ill*. Tap again to take a mark off.
  The marks stay on the phone (they are not part of the backup).
- **What the journal says** (*Insights, Journal*). For every mark with at least four nights with it and four without, the night
  after a marked day is set against the nights after the other days: HRV, resting heart rate and sleep. A difference that is
  big enough to matter (HRV 5 percent, resting heart rate 2 beats, sleep 24 minutes) is coloured, red when the night was worse.
- **Heads-up** (from Oura's Symptom Radar and WHOOP's Health Monitor). When your resting heart rate is 4 or more beats above your
  own normal, or your HRV is 20 percent or more below it, for two days in a row, *Home* shows a card with the numbers. Both
  signals off, or three days in a row, is the stronger message. Your normal is the average of the four weeks before.

### Not verified

- Nothing ran on a phone; the screens are drawn and tested on the JVM, with the numbers made up by the tests.
- The limits (4 beats, 20 percent, two days, four nights) are rules of thumb, pinned by tests so they can be tuned.
- The journal shows what goes together, not what causes what, and a few weeks of notes say little. The heads-up is not a diagnosis
  and not medical advice.

### What changed in the code

- `core/Journal.kt` (new): `JournalTag`, `JournalBook` (toggle, a plain-text `encode`/`decode` that skips what it cannot read),
  `JournalInsights.effects` and `TagEffect` (with `notable` and `worse`).
- `core/EarlyWarning.kt` (new): `EarlyWarning.compute` (28-day baseline, last 3 days, at least 7 baseline days) and `BodyWarning`.
- `SettingsStore.journal` keeps the book like the shoes are kept; `HomeState` has `journal` and `warning`, `JournalState` and
  `loadJournal`/`toggleJournal` are in `AppViewModel`; `ui/JournalScreen.kt` (new) draws the card, the heads-up and the tab.
- Tests: `JournalTest`, `EarlyWarningTest` in `core`; `JournalScreenTest` (marking on Home, the tab, a tag with worse nights,
  the heads-up and its absence).
