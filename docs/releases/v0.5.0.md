## OpenConnect v0.5.0: a new look, a sync that behaves like Garmin Connect, and Gemini

A redesigned app with a new logo, syncing that runs by itself like the Garmin Connect app, **Google Gemini next to
Claude**, a **Coach** you can ask about your own numbers, and a lot of new things to look at.

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.5.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.4.0 and keeps your data and your keys** (same
signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.5.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **A new look.** "Arcade night": deep indigo, mint as the main colour, one colour per body signal (steps, heart,
  stress, Body Battery, sleep) and monospace numbers like an old scoreboard. Rings, tiles with 7-day lines, sport badges,
  a dark and a light theme, and a setting to follow the phone or pick one. Home now opens with a greeting, your level and
  the sync status, then a readiness ring, today's steps ring and the signal tiles.
- **A new logo and icon.** An activity ring that is open where a connection node sits, with a heartbeat through it. It
  comes with a themed (single-colour) icon for Android 13 and later.
- **Sync like in the Garmin app.** It syncs when the app comes to the front if the last sync is over 30 minutes old (it
  was 6 hours), in the background about every 3 hours (Android's WorkManager: needs a connection and a battery that is not
  low, survives a restart), and when you pull down on Home. A pill at the top of Home says *Synced 5 min ago*, how far a
  running sync is, or what went wrong, and a tap does the sensible thing. Automatic syncing is now **on** once a key is saved.
- **Gemini next to Claude.** Each has its own key, model and *Check key* button in Settings. Choose *Automatic* (Claude,
  else Gemini, else offline), *Offline only*, *Claude* or *Gemini*. The keys are only typed into the app, kept in
  Keystore-encrypted storage and sent only to their own provider, in a header. See [docs/AI.md](https://github.com/Tufein/OpenConnect/blob/main/docs/AI.md).
- **Coach.** *Insights, Coach*: a chat with the AI of your choice that sees a digest of your last four weeks (numbers and
  activity names, no GPS). It gives general guidance, says when the data is too thin and does not give medical advice.
- **Readiness.** A 0 to 100 estimate from sleep, Body Battery, HRV, resting heart rate and stress, against your own
  normal. It is OpenConnect's own estimate, not Garmin's score.
- **Trophies and a level.** 17 trophies ("Press Start", "5K Boss", "Save Point", "Warp Zone", ...) and a level with XP,
  worked out from your data and never stored.
- **Search and sort** in Activities, **CSV export** of your daily health and your activities, and one place (Insights) for
  the week, the trophies and the coach. Details: [docs/INSIGHTS.md](https://github.com/Tufein/OpenConnect/blob/main/docs/INSIGHTS.md).
- English and Dutch for everything above.

### What changed in the code

- **`core` (pure Kotlin).** `ReportProvider` now has `generate`, `answer` (a chat turn) and `checkKey`; the Claude provider
  was refactored onto it and `GeminiReportProvider` is new (`generateContent`, key in `x-goog-api-key`, thought parts
  skipped, blocked and truncated answers reported in words). New: `CoachService` and `coachFacts` (the digest),
  `ReadinessCalculator`, `AchievementsCalculator`, `ActivityQuery` (search and sort) and `Csv`. 53 new tests.
- **`app`.** `AiController` and `CoachController` have no Android types, like the Garmin and watch controllers (the
  network sits behind `HttpTransport`, the keys behind `AiSettings`). `SettingsStore` keeps the keys (the v0.4 Claude key
  keeps its name). `GarminController.syncInBackground` runs the same short sync as the screen and returns an outcome the
  background job turns into success, retry or stop. New: `BackgroundSync` (WorkManager), `SyncBadge`, the new theme and
  components, and the Insights, Trophies, Coach and Settings screens.
- **Android.** WorkManager adds four permissions to the app: network state, wake lock, receive boot completed (to
  schedule again after a restart) and foreground service (declared by the library, not used). The launcher icon is a vector
  with a monochrome layer. Version 0.5.0, version code 5.

### How it was verified

Test first. The core has 228 tests (53 new), the app 138 (66 new), the Python server 230; lint is clean. The new screens are
driven end to end on the JVM with a fake Claude and Gemini: pasting a key, the check, a refused key, the coach's answer, what
is and is not in the request, trophies, readiness, the sync pill, search and the theme, in English and Dutch. Several tests
were verified by breaking the code on purpose. Looking at the rendered screens found a real layout bug (a button squeezed
into a thin strip on a narrow phone); it is fixed and has a test.

### Known limitations: please read

- **Gemini and the coach have not been tried with a real key.** The Claude request is the one used since v0.1.0, and the
  Gemini request follows Google's documented shape, but no real Claude or Gemini key was used in this release. Model names
  change at the providers; the model field is editable.
- **Still not tried on a real phone:** the new look on real screens, the background job (Android decides when it runs; it
  can be late in battery saver), USB and MTP, the folder picker, Bluetooth and Android's Keystore. Tests use fakes.
- **The watch must have synced with the Garmin Connect app first.** OpenConnect reads Garmin's cloud, so a sync here only
  shows what the watch already handed over.
- **Readiness is an estimate, not Garmin's Training Readiness**, and not medical advice. The coach likewise.
- The coach sends numbers and activity names to the AI you chose. Choose *Offline only* to send nothing.
- Spreadsheets with Dutch settings need the comma set as separator when opening the CSV.
- The business tier of the Python server (OAuth, push) still has not been run against Garmin.
