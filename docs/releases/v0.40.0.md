## OpenConnect v0.40.0: live heart rate keeps recording with the screen off

The first item from the roadmap's "later" list: a **foreground service** so a live heart-rate recording keeps running
once the screen goes off.

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.40.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.39.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.40.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Live heart rate keeps recording with the screen off** (*Watch, Live heart rate*). As soon as a heart-rate sensor is
  streaming, OpenConnect shows a **persistent notification** with the current heart rate and the elapsed time. The
  notification is what keeps the process (and so the Bluetooth connection) alive, so a training carries on with the
  phone in a pocket or on a table. The notification has a **Stop** action that ends the recording there and then;
  stopping in the app works as before. Whatever was recorded stays on the Watch screen and can be saved as an activity.
  When the recording ends (you stop it, or the sensor drops out for good), the notification goes away by itself.

### Not verified

- Nothing ran on a phone. The notification and its start/stop wiring are drawn and tested on the JVM; how Android
  treats the foreground service on a real device (Doze, battery saver, background-start limits, Android 14's
  *connected-device* type) is not. The recording itself still needs a real watch or chest strap, as before.

### What changed in the code

- `watch/LiveRecordingService.kt` (new): the foreground service, the notification (channel, bpm and elapsed time, a
  Stop action) and `ForegroundLiveRecording`, the real `LiveRecordingKeeper`.
- `watch/WatchLinks.kt`: the `LiveRecordingKeeper` interface and `NoLiveRecordingKeeper`, so the controller stays free
  of Android types and tests can fake the notification.
- `watch/WatchController.kt`: a `keeper` parameter; the live state is watched so the notification appears while
  streaming or reconnecting and disappears when the recording ends, on every path (stop, and the last failed retry).
- `OpenConnectApp.kt` wires `ForegroundLiveRecording`; the manifest declares the foreground-service permissions and the
  `connectedDevice` service type.
- Tests: `WatchControllerTest` (4) for the notification lifecycle and its Stop action, `LiveRecordingServiceTest` (3)
  for the notification text and shape. Strings in English and Dutch.
