## OpenConnect v0.28.0: shoes and their kilometres

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.28.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.27.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.28.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **A Shoes tab under Insights.** Add a pair (a name and a sport: running, walking, hiking or cycling) and see how far it
  has gone: kilometres against its limit as a bar, the number of activities and the day of the last one.
  - **Which activity goes on which pair:** each activity goes on the **newest pair of its sport that was in use that
    day**. A new pair is the default from today on; tick *Count my earlier activities too* and it also takes the
    activities from before (when no other pair of that sport has them). So a second pair does not rewrite the history of
    the first.
  - **Replace-after limit:** 800 km by default, moved in steps of 50 km (between 100 and 3000) with *Limit −50 km* and
    *Limit +50 km*. Under 80 percent a pair is *in good shape*, from 80 percent *getting old*, from the limit *time for a
    new pair*, and a card at the top of the tab lists the pairs that are there.
  - **Retire** takes a pair out of use from today: its past stays with it, new activities go to the next pair. *Back in
    use* undoes it; *Remove* deletes the pair.
- **Per activity:** on an activity's page, *Shoes on this activity* shows the pair and lets you pick another pair of that
  sport, *No shoes* or *Follow the default*. Only that one activity changes.
- **A notification** (when notifications are on in Settings): once per pair and limit, from 08:00, when a pair in use
  reaches its limit: "Pegasus has done 812 km: time to think about a new pair." Raising the limit makes the next one
  fresh news.

### Not verified

- Nothing here ran on a phone. The rules are covered by unit tests and the screens by UI tests on the JVM.
- Garmin Connect has a gear feature of its own; OpenConnect does not read it. Kilometres here come from the activities in
  OpenConnect, so a pair is only as complete as your synced history.

### What changed in the code

- `core/Gear.kt` (new): `Shoe`, `GearBook` (stored as text: one line per pair and per hand-made choice, awkward names
  escaped, unreadable lines skipped), `ShoeStatus`, `ShoeUsage` and `Gear` (`add`, `retire`, `resume`, `moveLimit`,
  `remove`, `assign`, `shoeFor`, `usage`, `status`, `due`, `choices`).
- `core/Nudges.kt`: a `SHOES_WORN` nudge; `notify/NudgeService.kt` works out the worn pairs and writes the message.
- `core/GearTest.kt` and `NudgesShoesTest.kt`: the text round trip and damaged lines, the "newest pair of the day"
  rule with starts, retirements, other sports and hand-made choices, the kilometres, the 80 and 100 percent edges, the
  limit range, and the nudge hours and keys; `NudgeServiceTest` gets a case for the whole path.
- App: `SettingsStore.gear`, `AppViewModel.loadGear` and `gearAction`, `GearScreen.kt` (new), the Shoes tab in
  `InsightsScreen.kt`, the shoes card in `ActivitiesScreens.kt`, strings in English and Dutch; `GearScreenTest` adds a pair,
  moves its limit, retires and removes it, and puts an activity on no shoes and back.

### Samenvatting (Nederlands)

Onder Inzichten staat nu het tabblad Schoenen: voeg een paar toe en zie hoeveel kilometer het al heeft gelopen, tegen een
grens van standaard 800 km (instelbaar). Elke activiteit hoort bij het nieuwste paar van zijn sport dat die dag in gebruik
was, zodat een nieuw paar de geschiedenis van het oude niet herschrijft; op de pagina van een activiteit kies je zelf een
ander paar. Bereikt een paar zijn grens, dan zie je dat in de app en (met meldingen aan) krijg je een melding.
