## OpenConnect v0.33.0: a sleek and a classic style next to retro

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.33.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.32.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.33.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

The retro look is not for everyone, so there is now a **style** to choose (*Settings, Appearance, Style*):

- **Retro**: the pixel look as before, with the choice between pixel letters and clear letters. It stays the default.
- **Sleek**: round corners, thin lines, no shadows, a smooth bar instead of block bars, a round switch, clear letters.
- **Classic**: small corners, thin lines, clear letters with serif titles and big numbers.

All 24 colour themes work in all three styles. The letters choice is only shown in Retro, since the other two always
use clear letters.

### Not verified

- Nothing ran on a phone; the styles are drawn and tested on the JVM (screenshots are in the CI run).
- The icons are still pixel icons in every style, and the dashed ring and the line charts are unchanged. If the pixel
  icons stand out in Sleek or Classic, a line-icon set is the next step.

### What changed in the code

- `ui/Theme.kt`: `Style` (`RETRO`, `CLEAN`, `CLASSIC`) and `LocalStyle`, two new `Shapes` sets, `ClassicTypography` (the
  clear letters with the phone's serif face for titles and numbers), `OpenConnectTheme(mode, palette, letters, style)`.
- `ui/Pixel.kt` and `ui/Components.kt`: `lineWidth()` (2 dp in retro, 1 dp otherwise) replaces the fixed outline width,
  `inkColor()` and `shadowColor()` follow the style, buttons, chips, the switch, `DottedRule` and `SegmentBar` have a
  smooth variant outside retro.
- `SettingsStore.style`, `Look.style`, `AppViewModel.setStyle`, the Style section in `SettingsScreen.kt`, strings in English and Dutch.
- Test: `sleekAndClassicStylesAreChosenAndKept`. `docs/DESIGN.md` has a "Styles" section.

### Samenvatting (Nederlands)

De retrostijl is niet voor iedereen, dus je kiest nu een **stijl** onder *Instellingen, Uiterlijk, Stijl*: **Retro** (zoals
voorheen, blijft de standaard), **Strak** (afgeronde hoeken, dunne lijnen, geen schaduw, duidelijke letters) of **Deftig**
(kleine hoeken, dunne lijnen, titels met schreef). Alle 24 kleurthema's werken in alle drie. De pixeliconen blijven in elke
stijl; dat en het kijken op een echte telefoon zijn nog niet gedaan.
