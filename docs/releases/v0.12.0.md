## OpenConnect v0.12.0: a home-screen widget and optional notifications

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.12.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.11.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.12.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Home-screen widget.** Long-press the home screen, Widgets, OpenConnect. It shows your readiness score, today's steps
  (against your step goal when the watch gives one) and how many activities you did this week against your weekly goal.
  Tap it to open the app. It redraws when new data arrives and about every 30 minutes.
- **Notifications (off until you switch them on in Settings).** On Android 13 and newer the app asks for permission.
  Checked about every three hours, each nudge is sent once:
  - **A goal is close:** from 17:00, a weekly goal at 80% or more but not reached (for activities: one missing).
  - **Monday summary:** from 08:00 on Monday, last week's activities, kilometres and goals reached, when there was any.
  - **Load jump:** once a week, when the training load estimate reads "big jump".

### What changed in the code

- `core/Nudges.kt`: the rules, pure and tested (`NudgesTest`: thresholds, once-only keys, Monday, load, key list capped at
  40). `core/WidgetSummary.kt`: what the widget shows.
- `notify/NudgeService.kt`: runs the rules against the data, remembers the keys *before* showing (so a crash cannot repeat a
  nudge), `AndroidNudgeNotifier` posts the notification, `NudgeWorker` / `WorkManagerNudgeSync` run it every three hours.
- `widget/HomeWidget.kt` with `HomeWidgetViews` (tested by building the views and reading the text back), a layout, a
  provider description and a manifest entry. The container redraws widgets after data changes, and only works out the
  summary when a widget is actually on the home screen.
- Settings got a Notifications card with the switch and the permission request.
- Fixed while testing: the first version started the widget refresh before the data-changed flow existed, which would have
  crashed the app at start. The UI tests caught it before release.

### Honest limits

- Widget and notifications are checked with unit and UI tests; how they look and when Android fires the job has to be
  seen on a phone. The widget is not shown in a screenshot because the test environment cannot draw widgets.
- Times are not exact: Android may run the three-hourly job later in battery saver or Doze.
- Readiness, load and the goal thresholds are OpenConnect's own rules of thumb, not medical advice.
- Health Connect was planned for this release and moved to v0.13.0.
