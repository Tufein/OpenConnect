## OpenConnect v0.37.0: how monotonous your week was, and how long to sleep tonight

Fourth release from the app comparison in [APP-VERGELIJKING.md](../APP-VERGELIJKING.md) (idea 5, and the sleep need from the "later" list).

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.37.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.36.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.37.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Monotony and strain** (*Insights, Form*, under the charts; Foster's method as used by Intervals.icu and TrainingPeaks). Seven days
  of training load: *monotony* is the mean daily load divided by its spread, so a week with the same load every day scores high and
  a week of hard days, easy days and rest scores low. *Strain* is the week's load times the monotony. You get a plain verdict
  (*Varied week*, *A bit uniform*, *Very monotonous*), the two numbers, the rest days of the week and your usual strain (the average of
  the four weeks before).
- **Sleep tonight** (*Home*, after your day so far). A target for tonight in the spirit of WHOOP's sleep need: eight hours, 15 or 30
  minutes more after a day with a load of 150 or 300, plus a quarter of your sleep debt of the last seven nights (at most an hour).
  The card shows last night and says whether the debt is none, small (1 hour or more) or big (4 hours or more). It needs four nights
  with sleep from your watch.

### Not verified

- Nothing ran on a phone; the screens are drawn and tested on the JVM.
- The verdict limits (monotony under 1.5 varied, under 2.0 uniform) and the sleep numbers (8 hours, 15 and 30 minutes, a quarter of the
  debt) are rules of thumb, pinned by tests so they can be tuned. Your own sleep need may be different: it is a suggestion, not medical advice.
- The load is OpenConnect's own estimate (minutes times an effort weight from heart rate), not TSS.

### What changed in the code

- `core/Recovery.kt` (new): `MonotonyCalculator.compute` (population standard deviation over seven days, monotony capped at 5, the usual strain
  from the four weeks before), `SleepNeedCalculator.compute`, `Recovery.dayLoad`.
- `HomeState` has `monotony` and `sleepNeed`, filled by `loadHome`; `ui/RecoveryCards.kt` (new) draws `MonotonyCard`, `MonotonyTiles` and
  `SleepNeedCard`; `FormContent` takes the monotony. Strings in English and Dutch.
- Tests: `RecoveryTest` (12) in `core`, `RecoveryScreenTest` (2) for the cards.
