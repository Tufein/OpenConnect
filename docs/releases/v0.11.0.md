## OpenConnect v0.11.0: archive on the watch and Bluetooth in the background (both optional, both unproven on a real watch)

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.11.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.10.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.11.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

Two switches under *Connect watch, Bluetooth*. **Both are off until you turn them on.**

- **Mark files as synced on the watch.** After a Bluetooth sync has stored the files, OpenConnect sends the same
  "archive" flag that Garmin Connect sends. Only files that were stored are marked: files that failed to read, and a
  cancelled sync, mark nothing. The watch may then **delete those files to make room**, so make a backup first (Settings,
  Back up) and turn it on only if this app is the one that reads the watch; Garmin Connect may then skip those files.
  OpenConnect waits for the watch to confirm every mark, so the link is not closed while marks are still on their way.
- **Look for the watch in the background (experimental).** About every three hours, with the app closed, Android runs a job
  that connects to the watch you already synced once and reads what is new. It never searches or pairs by itself, needs the
  Bluetooth permission, tries again twice (15 minutes apart) when the watch is out of range, and every attempt shows up in
  the sync log from v0.10.0. Android may delay it in battery saver or Doze.

### What changed in the code

- `WatchSource.archive(files)` (default: marks nothing). `WatchSyncService` calls it after everything is written, with the
  files that were stored (read now, or skipped because an earlier sync stored them), never after a cancel, and a failure
  there becomes a line in the report instead of failing the sync. `WatchSyncReport.archived` counts what was marked.
- `GarminBleWatchSource(archiveAfterRead)` sends `GfdiOut.archive(index)` for those files, and stops at the first lost link.
- `WatchController.syncGarminInBackground()` and `GarminBleSyncWorker` / `WorkManagerBleBackgroundSync` (WorkManager).
  The job is scheduled when the switch is on and cancelled when it is off; it is also rescheduled at app start.
- Settings: `GarminBleOptions` (two booleans, off by default). Tests: archive rules in `WatchSyncTest`, the Bluetooth path
  against the pretend watch in `GarminBleWatchSourceTest`, switches and background outcomes in `GarminBleControllerTest`.

### Not built, and why

- **The newer protobuf file sync** and a **live heart rate foreground service**. Both can only be built and checked against a
  real watch, and I will not ship protocol code I cannot test. They stay on `docs/ROADMAP.md`.
- The background job has only been tested against pretend watches. Whether Android lets it connect with the screen off, and
  how often, has to be seen on a phone with your fenix 8. The sync log will show what happened.
