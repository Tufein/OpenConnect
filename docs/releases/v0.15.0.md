## OpenConnect v0.15.0: training plan and app lock

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.15.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.14.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.15.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Training plan.** *Insights, Plan*: choose 5K or 10K, 2 to 4 runs a week and 6 to 12 weeks. The plan starts next Monday
  from the distance you run now (average of the last four weeks). You see the next run with a short description, this week's
  runs, and every week as a bar of planned against run distance (*Done* at 80% or more, *Behind* if not). The plan is
  rebuilt from your choice every time, so it never goes stale; *Delete plan* removes it.
- **App lock.** *Settings, App lock*: when on, OpenConnect asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock when it opens
  and after it has been out of sight for a minute (so picking a file or answering Health Connect does not lock you again).
  The recent-apps list and screenshots stay blank. You must pass the check to switch it on, and a phone without any screen
  lock simply opens the app rather than lock you out.

### What changed in the code

- `core/TrainingPlan.kt`: `PlanConfig` (the only thing stored), `TrainingPlans.build`, `baselineKm`, `progress`.
  Weekly distance grows by at most 10%, every fourth week is 75%, the last week is 60% and ends with the race on a Sunday;
  quality runs alternate tempo and intervals. 11 tests including the lock rule.
- `core/AppLock.kt`: `LockGate`, the pure rule for when the app is locked (start, and away longer than the grace period).
- `lock/Unlocker.kt`: Android 11+ uses the system biometric prompt with PIN fallback; Android 8 to 10 use the screen lock's
  confirm screen. Nothing is stored. `MainActivity` shows `LockScreen` instead of the app while locked and sets
  `FLAG_SECURE` while the lock is on. Tests use a fake check (`PlanAndLockScreenTest`).
- New permission `USE_BIOMETRIC` (a normal one, granted at install, shown by Android as no prompt).
- A bug the UI tests caught before release: the check asked Android for a system service before `onCreate`, which would
  have crashed the app at start.
- Version 0.15.0, versionCode 15.

### Known limits

- The plan is a rule of thumb used by many beginner plans, not medical advice. It does not know your health, injuries or
  pace; the tempo and interval sessions have no target pace.
- The real fingerprint or face check has not been tried on a phone (tests use a fake). The widget and the notifications
  still show their short summaries while the app is locked.
- The Bluetooth sync and Health Connect are still untested on real hardware.
