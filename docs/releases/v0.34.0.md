## OpenConnect v0.34.0: fitness, fatigue and form, and a training advice for today

This is the first release from the app comparison in [APP-VERGELIJKING.md](../APP-VERGELIJKING.md) (ideas 1 and 6).

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.34.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.33.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.34.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Fitness, fatigue and form** (*Insights, Form*). The classic picture from TrainingPeaks and Intervals.icu, without a power
  meter: your daily training load (the minutes times an effort weight from heart rate that the weekly load card already uses)
  is averaged slowly over 42 days (*fitness*) and quickly over 7 days (*fatigue*). The difference is your *form*: above zero you
  are rested, below zero you carry fatigue. You get a block bar like a health bar, a plain verdict (*Fresh*, *In balance*,
  *Building*, *Strained*), the three numbers and three charts over the last 90 days. It needs two weeks of activities.
- **Training advice on Home**, under the readiness card: *Take a rest day*, *Keep it easy*, *Train as planned* or *Good day for a
  hard one*. The weaker of the readiness estimate and the form status decides, and the card says which of the two holds you back.
  Tap it to open the Form tab.

### Not verified

- Nothing ran on a phone; the screens are drawn and tested on the JVM.
- The thresholds (form of 20 percent of your fitness or more is *Fresh*, down to minus 5 percent is *In balance*, down to minus
  30 percent is *Building*, below that *Strained*) are rules of thumb, not science. They are pinned by tests so they can be tuned.
- Both are OpenConnect's own estimates, in their own units: not TSS, not Garmin's Training Status, not medical advice.

### What changed in the code

- `core/Form.kt` (new, pure Kotlin): `FormCalculator.compute` turns the activities into one `FormPoint` per day with two
  exponential averages (`fitness += (load - fitness) / 42`, `fatigue += (load - fatigue) / 7`), starting on the day of the
  first activity, counting days without activity as zero load and ignoring activities in the future. `FormStatus.of` judges form
  against fitness. `TodayAdviceCalculator.compute` takes the minimum of two small scales (readiness level, form status).
- `core/TrainingLoad.kt` is unchanged; `activityLoad` is reused.
- `HomeState` has `form` and `advice`, filled by `loadHome` (the activity list is now read once for both). `ui/FormScreen.kt`
  (new) has the `AdviceCard` and the Form tab; `HomeScreen`, `InsightsScreen` (new `InsightsTab.Form`) and `Navigation` wire it
  in. Strings in English and Dutch.
- Tests: `FormTest` (11) and `TodayAdviceTest` (4) in `core`, `FormScreenTest` (2) for the screens.

### Samenvatting (Nederlands)

Nieuw onder *Inzichten, Vorm*: **fitheid, vermoeidheid en vorm**, zoals TrainingPeaks en Intervals.icu dat laten zien, maar
zonder powermeter. Je trainingsbelasting per dag wordt traag gemiddeld over 42 dagen (fitheid) en snel over 7 dagen
(vermoeidheid). Het verschil is je vorm: boven nul ben je uitgerust, onder nul draag je vermoeidheid mee. Je ziet een
levensbalk, een duidelijk oordeel (*Fris*, *In balans*, *Aan het opbouwen* of *Zwaar belast*), drie cijfers en drie grafieken
over 90 dagen. Op Home staat onder je herstelscore nu een **trainingsadvies** voor vandaag: rustdag, rustig, zoals gepland of
een goede dag voor een zware training. Het zwakste van herstel en vorm beslist, en de kaart zegt welk van de twee je afremt.
Alles is een eigen schatting, geen medisch advies, en nog niet op een echte telefoon bekeken.
