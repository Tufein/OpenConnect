## OpenConnect v0.7.0: charts that explain themselves, and living next to Garmin Connect

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.7.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.6.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.7.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Charts with an explanation.** Under every chart on *Health* (steps, sleep, resting heart rate, Body Battery, HRV,
  SpO2, respiration, stress) the app now writes:
  - the period the chart covers;
  - a verdict in colour: *In a good range*, *Fair* or *Worth watching*;
  - the average, with the lowest and highest day and their dates;
  - the trend: the last 7 readings against the 7 before, in percent, and whether that is better or worse;
  - for steps, how many days reached 10,000;
  - one or two sentences on what the number means and which range is normal.
  The heart-rate and elevation charts of an activity get their average, highest and range too. The ranges are general
  guides, not medical advice; HRV is judged against your own average because it is personal.
- **Using it next to Garmin Connect.** A watch talks to one phone app at a time over Bluetooth, so both apps cannot sync
  the watch at once. The Watch screen now says so and points to what does work: leave Garmin Connect as the watch's app
  and let OpenConnect read the same data from your Garmin account, or from a cable or folder.
- **No doubles.** A run that arrives from the watch and from the Garmin account is recognised by its start time and kept
  once, in either order (the account route already did this; the watch route now does too).

### What changed in the code

- New `core/ChartInsight.kt`: `ChartInsight.of(metric, series)` computes average, extremes with dates, trend, verdict
  and goal days from the same series that is drawn. 11 tests.
- New `ui/ChartInsightUi.kt`: `InsightCaption`, used by every card in `HealthScreen.kt`; captions on the activity charts.
- `WatchSyncService`: looks for an activity within 2 minutes of the start time before adding one. 1 new test.
- Watch screen: a card "Using Garmin Connect too?"; the connect error hints at the account route. English and Dutch.
- Version 0.7.0, versionCode 7.

### Known limits

- The Bluetooth sync is still untested on a real watch.
- The ranges are population guides; they do not know your age, training or health.
