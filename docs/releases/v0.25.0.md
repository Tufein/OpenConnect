## OpenConnect v0.25.0: race predictions and a race goal

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.25.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.24.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.25.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Predicted race times** for 5 km, 10 km, the half marathon and the marathon, worked out from your best efforts with
  Riegel's formula (`t2 = t1 * (d2 / d1) ^ 1.06`). Each line says what it rests on: *your best* (you ran that distance),
  *good guess* (up to 2.5 times the distance you ran), *rough guess* (up to 4.5 times) or *only a hint* (further than
  that, such as a marathon from a 5 km). Garmin's own Race Predictor is not offered to apps, so this is OpenConnect's
  own estimate.
- **A race goal** at the top of *Insights, Plan*: pick a distance, *Set race goal*, and you get a date twelve weeks away
  and a target time (your predicted time, rounded to half a minute). The card shows the countdown, the pace you need and a
  verdict: *ahead* (predicted at least 2 percent faster), *right on the target* (within 2 percent) or *needs more work*.
  The date moves by a week and the target by a minute with the buttons; *Remove race goal* clears it.

### Not verified

- Nothing here ran on a phone. The maths is covered by unit tests and the screen by a UI test on the JVM.
- Predictions need runs with a track of at least 4 km (that is where best efforts come from).
- Riegel's formula is a rule of thumb: it knows nothing about your training, the course or the weather.

### What changed in the code

- `core/Races.kt` (new): `RaceDistance`, `RacePredictor` (scaling, closest effort as the base, reliability), `RaceGoal`
  (stored as `date|distance|seconds`, refuses bad text), `Races.outlook` and `Races.state`.
- `core/RacesTest.kt`: the formula against a hand-worked value, the choice of base effort, the reliability limits, the
  goal round trip and refusals, the 2 percent verdict edges, the day count and the case without data.
- App: `SettingsStore.raceGoal`, `PlanState.race`, `AppViewModel.raceAction` (start, shift date, shift target, clear),
  the race card in `PlanScreen.kt` and strings in English and Dutch; a UI test sets, moves and removes a goal.

### Samenvatting (Nederlands)

Op het tabblad Plan staan nu voorspelde racetijden (5 km, 10 km, halve en hele marathon) uit je beste prestaties, met
de formule van Riegel en een eerlijke aanduiding hoe betrouwbaar elke schatting is. Je kunt een racedoel instellen met
datum en streeftijd: de app telt af, laat het benodigde tempo zien en zegt of je voor ligt, op koers zit of meer werk
moet doen. Dit is OpenConnects eigen schatting, niet die van Garmin.
