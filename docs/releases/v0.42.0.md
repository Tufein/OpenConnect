## OpenConnect v0.42.0: three small cards on Home, sleep rhythm, resting heart rate trend and step goal week

Second release of the "many small releases before midnight" run. Three small, well-tested ideas from the "later" list in
[APP-VERGELIJKING.md](../APP-VERGELIJKING.md) (what Oura, WHOOP and Garmin Connect each show), shipped together because the time
left before midnight did not allow one release for each.

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.42.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.41.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.42.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new (all on Home)

- **Sleep rhythm** (under the sleep target for tonight). How alike the length of your last 14 nights was: the average night, the spread in
  minutes (the standard deviation of the night lengths), your shortest and longest night, and one of three verdicts: *Steady nights*
  (under 30 minutes of spread), *Some ups and downs* (30 up to 60) and *Long and short nights swing* (60 and more). Needs 7 nights with
  data in those 14 days.
- **Resting heart rate trend** (under the early warning). The average resting heart rate of your last 7 days against the average of the
  28 days before. *Lower than your normal* (2 beats or more lower: usually good recovery and growing fitness), *Right on your normal*, or
  *Higher than your normal* (2 beats or more higher: often tiredness, stress, heat, alcohol or the start of an illness). Needs 4 days with
  a value in the last week and 10 in the 28 days before. It is the quieter sibling of the early warning, which only speaks up at 4 beats
  and 2 days in a row.
- **Step goal this week** (under the steps card). On how many of the last 7 days you reached your step goal (*Reached on 5 of 7 days*), as
  a bar of blocks, with your average, your busiest day and the goal used. The goal is the one your watch reports for each day; when it
  reports none, 10,000 is used. Days without steps are left out; with fewer than 3 days with steps the card stays away.

A card whose data is not there yet (too few nights, days or values) stays away.

### Not verified

- Nothing ran on a phone; the cards are drawn and tested on the JVM with demo data.
- OpenConnect does not store bedtimes, only the length of a night, so the sleep rhythm is a consistency of length, not of the time you go to
  bed. The limits (30 and 60 minutes, 2 beats, the 10,000 fallback) are rules of thumb. Not medical advice.

### What changed in the code

- `core/SleepConsistency.kt` (new): `SleepConsistencyCalculator.compute(sleeps, today)` takes the nights of the last 14 days with a duration,
  needs at least 7, and returns average, spread in minutes, shortest, longest, number of nights and a `SleepRhythm` (STEADY, VARIABLE,
  ERRATIC; the edges belong to the higher verdict).
- `core/RestingHrTrend.kt` (new): `RestingHrTrendCalculator.compute(dailies, today)` averages the days with a resting heart rate above 0 in
  the last 7 days and in the 28 days before them (days after today and older days are ignored) and returns a `RestingHrTrend` (recent,
  normal, difference and a `RestingHrDirection` LOWER, STABLE or HIGHER; the edge of 2 beats belongs to LOWER and HIGHER).
- `core/StepGoalWeek.kt` (new): `StepGoalWeekCalculator.compute(dailies, today)` takes the days of the last 7 with steps above 0, needs at
  least 3, counts the days whose steps reach that day's own goal (or 10,000), and returns days reached, days with data, the goal of the
  latest day, the rounded average, the busiest day and its steps.
- `AppViewModel.loadHome` reads 14 nights, 35 days of daily summaries and 7 days into `HomeState.sleepConsistency`, `restingHrTrend` and
  `stepGoalWeek`; `HomeScreen` shows `SleepConsistencyCard`, `RestingHrTrendCard` and `StepGoalWeekCard` (new, in `ui/RecoveryCards.kt`; the
  step card uses the existing `SegmentBar`). Strings in English and Dutch.
- Tests: `SleepConsistencyTest` (6), `RestingHrTrendTest` (6) and `StepGoalWeekTest` (6) in `core`; `SleepRhythmScreenTest`,
  `RestingTrendScreenTest` and `StepWeekScreenTest` (1 each) for the cards. The ramp card test of v0.41.0 matches a unique sentence now.
