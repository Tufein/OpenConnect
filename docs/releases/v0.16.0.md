## OpenConnect v0.16.0: Oura Ring, one AI at a time, livelier screens

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.16.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.15.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.16.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Oura Ring.** *Settings, Oura Ring*: enter your own Oura application (client ID and secret; Oura has no personal
  tokens any more), log in at Oura in the browser, and the last 30 days come in: steps, active calories, intensity
  minutes, sleep with its score, resting heart rate, HRV, breathing, blood oxygen and workouts. Only gaps are filled, so
  nothing your watch gave is overwritten or counted twice. Setup in `docs/OURA.md`. Needs an active Oura Membership.
- **One AI at a time.** *Settings, AI*: offline, Claude or Gemini. Only the chosen AI's card is shown and only it is used;
  the other one is out of sight (a key saved for it earlier stays stored but unused until you pick it again). The old
  *Automatic* choice becomes the AI that has a key.
- **Sync on opening and every 10 minutes.** The Garmin account and the Oura ring sync when the app comes to the front and
  every 10 minutes while it stays open. In the background they run about every 15 minutes, the shortest period Android
  allows for a background job (it was three hours for Garmin).
- **Tiles you can tap.** On Home, the readiness card, the steps card and the resting heart rate, Body Battery, stress and
  sleep tiles open their chart on the Health screen; distance and activities open the activity list; goals open the week.
  On Health the four score tiles jump to their chart; on Insights the tiles do the same. A small arrow marks what can be
  tapped.
- **Charts you can read by touch.** Tap or slide a finger along a chart on Health (and the steps chart on Home): the line
  above it shows that day and its value, the day is marked, and in bar charts the other bars step back.

### What changed in the code

- `core/oura/OuraApi.kt`: `OuraAuth` (authorization code with PKCE S256 and state, token exchange, single-use refresh
  tokens), `OuraClient` (v2 collections with `next_token` paging, one fresh token on a 401, 403/429 told apart).
  `core/oura/OuraSync.kt`: maps Oura's documents to days, nights and activities and only fills gaps. 9 tests, including
  the PKCE example of RFC 7636.
- App: `oura/OuraController.kt`, `OuraRedirectActivity` (catches `openconnect://oura`), `ui/OuraScreen.kt`, the secrets in
  `SettingsStore.oura` (encrypted), an Oura WorkManager job, the sync log source `oura`.
- `AiController`: the choices are offline, Claude and Gemini; only the chosen one is ever used. `SettingsScreen` shows
  only that one's card.
- `AppViewModel.onResume/onPause`: syncs on arrival and runs a 10-minute timer while the app is in front.
  `GarminController.AUTO_SYNC_EVERY` is 10 minutes; the Garmin job is moved once from 3 hours to 15 minutes.
- `Components.kt`: `ScoreTile`, `StatTile` and `MetricTile` take `onClick` and show an arrow; `Charts.kt`: `LineChart`
  and `BarChart` take `labels` and `format` and can be read by touch. `HealthScreen` scrolls to a chart it is asked for.
- Tests: `OuraTest` (core), `OuraAndTouchScreenTest` (login with a fake Oura, tile to chart, touch reading), updated AI
  tests. Version 0.16.0, versionCode 16.

### Known limits

- Oura was only tested against a fake. The real login, Oura's answers and the browser coming back to the app have not
  been tried; Oura's terms limit unapproved applications to a few users, which is fine for your own use.
- Every 10 minutes holds only while the app is open. In the background Android allows 15 minutes at best and often
  waits longer (battery saver, Doze). More often would need a permanent notification (a foreground service).
- Garmin's Personal API is now asked more often (each sync reads 7 days); if Garmin starts refusing with "too many
  requests", the sync log shows it.
