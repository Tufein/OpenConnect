## OpenConnect v0.27.0: heart-rate zones

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.27.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.26.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.27.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **A Zones tab under Insights** (the tab chips now wrap onto a second line on a narrow phone).
  - **Maximum heart rate:** by default the highest heart rate in your activities (capped at 230, because a wrist sensor
    sometimes spikes). If you know your own maximum, set it with the *−5 bpm* and *+5 bpm* buttons; *Use the highest
    measured* goes back. A typed-in maximum must be between 120 and 230.
  - **Zone limits** as a percentage of that maximum, the same scheme Garmin uses: zone 1 is 50 to 60 percent, zone 2
    60 to 70, zone 3 70 to 80, zone 4 80 to 90, zone 5 90 to 100. The table shows the beats per minute for each.
  - **Last 7 days:** time in each zone as a bar, in minutes and as a share of the time in zones 1 to 5, with a plain
    verdict. It needs half an hour of heart rate in those 7 days. *Mostly easy with some hard work* is the good mix;
    over 30 percent in zones 4 and 5 is *a lot of hard work*; over 40 percent in zone 3 is *too much in the middle*;
    under 5 percent hard is *all easy, add one hard session*.
  - **Earlier weeks:** the three 7-day blocks before it, with the time in zones and the number of activities.
- **Every activity** now has a *Time in heart-rate zones* card under its heart-rate chart.
- **How the time is counted:** each heart-rate sample counts for the seconds until the next one, with a pause of more
  than 15 seconds counting for only 15. An activity without samples but with an average heart rate counts all its time
  in the average's zone and says so (a guess). Time below 50 percent is kept apart and not part of the shares.

### Not verified

- Nothing here ran on a phone. The maths is covered by unit tests, the screens by UI tests on the JVM.
- These are OpenConnect's own zones from a percentage of the maximum. Garmin's zones on your watch can follow your
  lactate threshold or heart-rate reserve and then differ. Not medical advice.

### What changed in the code

- `core/HeartZones.kt` (new): `MaxHr`, `ZoneRange`, `ZoneTimes`, `ActivityZones`, `WeekZones`, `ZoneSplit`,
  `ZoneVerdict`, and `HeartZones` (`maxHr`, `limits`, `zoneOf`, `ranges`, `timeInZones`, `activityZones`, `weeks`,
  `split`, `verdict`, `overview`).
- `core/HeartZonesTest.kt`: the limits and the zone edges, the maximum from setting, measured and capped values, the
  per-second counting, gaps, missing samples, the guess from an average, the week blocks and their edges, the shares and
  every verdict including the exact 30, 5 and 40 percent edges.
- App: `SettingsStore.maxHr`, `AppViewModel.loadZones` and `zonesAction`, `ZonesScreen.kt` (new), the Zones tab in
  `InsightsScreen.kt`, the zones card in `ActivitiesScreens.kt`, strings in English and Dutch; `ZonesScreenTest` loads the
  demo data, moves the maximum and back, and opens an activity's zones.

### Samenvatting (Nederlands)

Onder Inzichten staat nu het tabblad Zones: je maximale hartslag (standaard de hoogste gemeten, zelf aan te passen),
de zonegrenzen als percentage daarvan, de tijd per zone in de laatste 7 dagen met een duidelijk oordeel (te veel in het
midden, te zwaar, alles rustig of een goede mix) en de eerdere weken. Elke activiteit toont ook zijn tijd per zone. Dit
zijn OpenConnects eigen zones, die van je horloge kunnen afwijken.
