## OpenConnect v0.14.0: month and year reports, best efforts, lap table

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.14.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.13.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.14.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Week, month and year.** *Insights* has three chips under the tabs. The arrows move one week, month or year back or
  forward. The tiles, the AI report card and the report text follow the period. The report compares with the period before
  and, offline, is written by rules in English or Dutch; with a Claude or Gemini key it is written by the model.
- **Best efforts.** The fastest 1 km, 5 km, 10 km, half marathon and marathon found inside your recorded runs (not only
  runs of that length), with the date; tapping a row opens that run.
- **Lap table.** An activity now shows a table per kilometre: time, pace, heart rate and climb, with a shorter last lap.
  Below it, the fastest stretches inside that run.

### What changed in the code

- `core/BestEfforts.kt`: `Lap`, `Effort`, `BestEfforts.efforts(track)` (sliding window over the track, speeds above 9 m/s
  are ignored as GPS noise), `laps(track)` and `personalBests(repo)` (latest 300 runs).
- `core/reports/ReportPeriod.kt` (week, month, year with start, end and shift), `PeriodFacts` in `Facts.kt`,
  `ReportService.periodReport(...)`, prompts and offline text for monthly and yearly reports (en, nl). Reports are cached
  per kind and period.
- App: `AppViewModel` gets `setReportPeriod`, a generalised `shiftWeek` and `efforts`; `InsightsScreen` the chips and the
  Best efforts card; `ActivitiesScreens` the lap table. Tests: `PeriodReportTest`, `BestEffortsTest`, `PeriodScreenTest`.
- Version 0.14.0, versionCode 14.

### Known limits

- Best efforts need a GPS track with timestamps; runs without one do not count. Pauses inside a run count as time.
- Screenshots come from UI tests with demo data. The Bluetooth sync and Health Connect are still untested on real hardware.
