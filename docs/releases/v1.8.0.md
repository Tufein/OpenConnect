## OpenConnect v1.8.0: the charge of your watch and ring, and workouts by distance

Two things that were missing from the daily loop: you could not see how full the watch or the ring was, and the workout
builder only counted minutes.

### Install

Download `openconnect-v1.8.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v1.7.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 9 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v1.8.0.apk.sha256`, or let
*Settings, Updates* fetch and verify it.

### What is new

- **Battery card on the Watch tab.** Two rows, *Watch* and *Ring*, each with the charge and when it was last reported
  ("updated 12 min ago"); a dash means nothing has been reported yet. The watch is asked once over Bluetooth right
  after the handshake, with the same protobuf question Gadgetbridge sends, and a watch that stays quiet for two
  seconds never holds up the sync. The ring's charge comes from Oura's `ring_battery_level` on every Oura sync; when
  Oura refuses that call the sync still succeeds and the card keeps its dash instead of showing a wrong number. WHOOP
  and Hume do not report a charge, so they are not on the card.
- **One extra Oura permission.** Oura does not publish which scope `ring_battery_level` needs; the community found it
  is `ring_configuration`, so the login now asks for that too (it covers the ring itself, nothing new about your
  body). Someone who logged in before this version logs in once more to grant it; until then the ring row shows a
  dash and the rest of the sync is unchanged. If Oura refuses the scope, the login page says so and nothing else
  changes.
- **Workouts by distance and pace.** The fast repeat can be a distance with a pace range: enter *1000* metres and
  *300-330* (seconds per kilometre, so 5:00–5:30) and the FIT file carries a distance step with a pace target instead
  of a timed step. Leaving the distance empty keeps the old timed step, and the total time is estimated from the slow
  end of the pace. Both fields are checked (100 m to 50 km, 2:00 to 20:00 per km, fast end first).
- **Edit a workout.** Every saved workout now has *Edit* next to *Delete*: the form opens with its values, and saving
  replaces that workout instead of adding a second one.
- The stored format grew three fields per workout. Lines written by v1.7.0 still read; a workout saved by v1.8.0 is
  simply skipped by an older version instead of being shown wrong.

### Not verified

- **No real watch, ring or phone was used.** The battery question and the answer parsing are covered by JVM tests
  against hand-built protobuf bytes, the Oura call by a fake Oura; whether a given watch model answers the question at
  all, and whether Oura accepts `ring_configuration` and grants `ring_battery_level` to a personal application, can
  only be seen on a device. A watch that does not answer, or an Oura token without the scope, simply shows a dash.
- The build was compiled and the core tests ran locally; the Android tests and lint run in CI, because this machine
  cannot run the Robolectric runtime.

### What changed in the code

- `core/watch/ble/GarminProtobuf.kt`: `BATTERY_QUESTION`, `batteryRequest()`, `batteryLevel()`, `accepted()` and
  `Varints.take()`.
- `core/watch/ble/GarminBleSession.kt`: asks once after `SYNC_READY`, `awaitBattery()` with a two-second cap.
- `watch/AndroidGarminBleLink.kt` and `OpenConnectApp.kt`: the answer is kept with the time it arrived.
- `core/oura/OuraApi.kt` + `OuraData.kt`: `OuraBattery` and `latestBattery()`; `OuraController` stores it after a sync
  and never fails the sync over it.
- `data/SettingsStore.kt`: `watchBattery`, `watchBatteryAt`, `ringBattery`, `ringBatteryAt`.
- `ui/BatteryCard.kt` (new), `AppViewModel.refreshBattery()`, `Navigation.kt`, `WatchScreen.kt`: the card.
- `core/WorkoutBook.kt`: `fastMeters`, `paceFastS`, `paceSlowS`, `fastStepS`, `Problem.PACE`/`FAST_METERS`, the three
  extra fields in the stored line (older lines still read).
- `ui/PlanScreen.kt`: distance and pace fields, *Edit*, and the distance summary line.
- Tests: protobuf battery bytes, Oura battery (sample, empty, impossible), the controller keeping the charge and
  surviving a refusal, workout distance/pace and old-line decoding, and the screen saving a distance workout and
  editing it.
- Version 1.8.0, versionCode 82.
