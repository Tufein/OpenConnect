## OpenConnect v0.4.0: a Garmin connection that really works

The app can now pull your own data from your **Garmin account**, next to the watch routes of v0.3.0. This one was run
**live against a real Garmin account** before release, not only against test files.

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.4.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.3.0 and keeps your data** (same signing key).
Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.4.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **Garmin account screen.** Make a personal key on Garmin's own page (the app opens it for you), paste it, and the
  app checks with Garmin that it works. Then *Sync now* pulls the last 7, 30 or 90 days of steps, distance, floors,
  calories, intensity minutes, heart rate, stress, Body Battery, breathing, SpO2, HRV and sleep, plus your
  activities **with their original FIT files** (route, charts, splits and reports work as for any imported file). Each data type
  reports on its own: found, nothing in this period, not understood, or failed. Optionally the app syncs by itself
  when it opens, at most every 6 hours.
- **Nothing is doubled or shrunk.** Counters keep the larger value, a longer night is kept, and an activity that came in
  over USB is recognised when it arrives from the account (same start time).
- **No approval and no server.** This is Garmin's Personal API: it is for your own data, you make the key yourself, and
  it stays encrypted on your phone. It is sent only to Garmin, only to get a 24-hour token. If a phone cannot store it
  safely, it is not stored at all.
- **Read a folder.** On the Watch screen you can pick a folder with watch files (the `GARMIN` folder, a copy, an export of
  another app). Android's folder picker also lists a plugged-in watch as a storage device.
- **Bluetooth reconnects by itself.** If the heart rate sensor drops out, the phone tries again up to five times; the
  recording keeps what it had and can still be saved.
- A read where nothing at all could be read (cable pulled, folder gone) is now reported as a failure instead of a
  "has been read" with a footnote.

### How the Bluetooth question was answered

The Bluetooth link of a Garmin watch gives other apps only the standard **Heart Rate Service**. Steps, sleep and
activities travel over Garmin's own protocol to Garmin's own app. The routes around that were checked and are
documented: the Garmin Health SDK and Companion SDK are for approved enterprise partners only, a Connect IQ companion
needs a watch app and Garmin Connect Mobile, Gadgetbridge's reverse engineering is AGPL (it cannot be copied into an MIT
project), and Health Connect needs Garmin Connect. So the app uses Bluetooth for live heart rate, the cable or a folder
for raw files, and the Garmin account for everything the watch already handed to Garmin. Details and sources:
[docs/GARMIN.md](https://github.com/Tufein/OpenConnect/blob/main/docs/GARMIN.md).

### What was verified live

On 2026-11-12, with the owner's own key: the token exchange, all six day-level data types for 130 days (daily
summaries, steps, stress and Body Battery for all 130 days, HRV and sleep for 129), the activity list, and one
activity's ZIP download, all through the real client code. A real Garmin FIT file was read by the app's importer, and
a replay of the real answers filled every day field the app has. That is how the field names in the mapping were
found; Garmin does not publish them. The earlier guesses were wrong in places and have been replaced.

### How it was built

Test first, in the pure Kotlin `core` module (HTTP behind an interface, token renewal, mapping, sync) and a controller
without Android types in the app. The Garmin fixtures have the real layouts with invented values. The core now has 175
tests and the app 72, including a UI test that pastes a key, syncs against a fake Garmin and checks what lands in the
database, controller tests for the retries and the folder read, and Dutch and English screens. Several tests were
verified by breaking the code on purpose to see that they fail.

### Known limitations: please read

- **Still not tried on a real phone:** USB and MTP, the folder picker, Bluetooth and the reconnection, and Android's
  Keystore storage of the key. Tests use fakes for those.
- **One account, one watch family.** Other models may leave sections empty or answer differently. The Garmin screen's
  *Copy technical details* gives names and counts, never your data or the key, to put in an issue.
- The watch must have **synced with the Garmin Connect app** before its newest data shows up in the account.
- Syncing runs when the app is open. A background service, and a foreground service to keep a live heart rate
  recording going with the screen off, are not built yet.
- Naps, hydration and the all-day heart rate and SpO2 curves of the Personal API are not read yet. Battery level and
  device info need Garmin's own Bluetooth protocol and are not possible.
- The business tier of the Python server (OAuth, push) still has not been run against Garmin.
