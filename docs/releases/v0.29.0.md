## OpenConnect v0.29.0: calendar and streaks

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.29.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.28.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.29.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **A Calendar tab under Insights** with the month as a grid, weeks starting on Monday. Each day with an activity is
  coloured (the longer the distance, the stronger) and shows its kilometres (or the number of activities for ones
  without a distance). The arrows page through the months; they stop at this month and at the first month with an
  activity.
- **Tap a day** to see how many activities it had and how far, and open any of them from there. A day without an
  activity says so.
- **Streaks** above the calendar, as four tiles: the *current streak* and the *longest streak* in days, and the *weeks
  in a row* and the *longest run of weeks* with at least one activity.
  - A streak is not broken until its day or week is over: if you were active yesterday but not yet today, the streak
    still counts and the screen says today is still open. The same goes for a week that has just begun.
  - Of two equally long runs, the most recent is the longest. Several activities on one day count once.
- Days are UTC days, like the rest of the app, and a week runs from Monday to Sunday.

### Not verified

- Nothing here ran on a phone. The rules are covered by unit tests, the screen by UI tests on the JVM.
- An activity that starts just after midnight in your time zone but before midnight UTC is counted on the earlier day.

### What changed in the code

- `core/Streaks.kt` (new): `DayItem`, `DayTotals`, `MonthView` (with the blanks before the 1st), `StreakRun`,
  `StreakSummary` and `Streaks` (`month`, `firstMonth`, `compute`).
- `core/StreaksTest.kt`: the month grouping and order, the weekday of the 1st, nameless activities, the first month,
  current and longest streaks, a streak still alive from yesterday and one that is over, one activity per day, ties,
  weeks from Monday, a week streak across a new week, and the empty case.
- App: `AppViewModel.loadCalendar` and `shiftCalendar`, `CalendarScreen.kt` (new), the Calendar tab in
  `InsightsScreen.kt`, strings in English and Dutch; `CalendarScreenTest` checks the streak tiles, paging to both ends,
  a day's activities and opening one.

### Samenvatting (Nederlands)

Onder Inzichten staat nu het tabblad Kalender: de maand als raster (weken beginnen op maandag) met per dag de afstand
en het aantal activiteiten, door de maanden te bladeren en een dag aan te tikken om zijn activiteiten te openen. Erboven
staan je reeksen: de huidige en de langste reeks dagen, en het aantal weken achter elkaar met beweging. Een reeks is pas
verbroken als de dag of week voorbij is.
