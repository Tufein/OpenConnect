## OpenConnect v0.32.0: more themes, easier to read, a launcher icon of your choice

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.32.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.31.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.32.0.apk.sha256`.

After the update the icon on your home screen may disappear once: the launcher entry now belongs to an icon you can
choose. Add the app again from the app drawer if that happens.

### What is new

v0.31.0 gave the app one look of its own, and it came with one real colour theme to speak of. That was too little, and the
pixel letters were hard to read for some. This release fixes both, and lets you pick the launcher icon.

- **24 colour themes** (*Settings, Appearance, Colours*) in four groups, each with a dark and a light version:
  - *Retro screens*: Handheld (the default), Game Boy, Virtual Boy, Famicom, Commodore 64, Atari, Amber terminal,
    Terminal and Synthwave. The one-colour screens (Game Boy, Virtual Boy, Terminal) keep to the shades of their colour.
  - *Easy on the eyes*: **High contrast** (pure black and white with a yellow or blue accent), Grey, Slate, Sand, Nord,
    Solarized, Gruvbox and Dracula.
  - *Colourful*: Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Candy, Violet and Cherry.
  - *Phone*: Wallpaper (the colours of your phone, Android 12 and newer).
- **Every theme is measured for readability.** A test holds all of them, dark and light, to the same limits: text on the
  background and on a panel at least 7 to 1, small grey text at least 4.5 to 1, text on every accent colour at least
  4.5 to 1 (the usual WCAG contrast ratios). A few colours of the old themes were adjusted to pass: the light Handheld
  orange and blue, the Game Boy text, the Virtual Boy red and the Gruvbox greys.
- **Clear letters** (*Settings, Appearance, Letters*): a plain, slightly larger face (IBM Plex Sans) for titles, numbers
  and small print instead of the pixel face. Everything else stays as it is. The default stays *Pixel letters*.
- **App icon** (*Settings, App icon*): eight icons to choose from: the heart on paper (the default), a night heart, a
  green Game Boy heart, an amber heart, a neon heart, a candy heart, a star and a stopwatch. Android 13 and newer also
  get a single-colour version for themed icons. The launcher may take a moment to show the new icon, and on some phones
  the app closes once while it changes.
- The tab bar starts with a smaller label size in clear letters and still shrinks until the longest word fits.

### Not verified

- Nothing here ran on a phone. The themes were drawn and measured by tests on the JVM (screenshots are in the CI run). How
  the colours feel on a real screen, in sunlight or at night, is not checked.
- Switching the launcher icon goes through Android's component switch. It is tested with a stand-in package manager,
  not on a real launcher: some launchers keep showing the old icon until they refresh.
- The themes' signal colours (heart, sleep, stress, Body Battery) are the same as before in every theme except the
  three one-colour screens.

### What changed in the code

- `ui/Theme.kt`: `Palette` now has 24 entries with a name and a group (`PaletteGroup`), `PaletteKeys` holds their dark and
  light key colours, `colorSchemeFor` and `paletteSwatch` expose them to Settings and tests, `Letters` and
  `ClearTypography` are the new letter choice (`OpenConnectTheme(mode, palette, letters)`), and the one-colour screens
  have their shades for the body signals.
- `ui/AppIcon.kt` (new): the eight `AppIcon`s, `AppIcons.current` and `AppIcons.select` (switch an activity alias on and
  the others off through the package manager) and the preview that Settings draws.
- `AndroidManifest.xml`: the launcher entry moved from `MainActivity` to eight `activity-alias` entries, one switched on.
  `res/drawable` and `res/mipmap-anydpi-v26` have the new icons, made by `android/tools/launcher_icons.py` from the
  pixel grids of the app.
- `ui/SettingsScreen.kt`: the themes in four groups with a two-colour swatch each, the Letters choice and the App icon
  section. `AppViewModel.kt`, `SettingsStore.kt`, `Navigation.kt` and `MainActivity.kt` carry the letters and the icon.
- Strings in English and Dutch for all of it.
- Tests: `ThemesLettersAndIconTest` (every theme's contrast, picking themes, clear letters kept, an icon chosen and the
  others switched off).
- `docs/DESIGN.md` describes the themes, the letters and the icons, and how to add a theme.

### Samenvatting (Nederlands)

Je vroeg om veel meer thema's, om iets dat beter leesbaar is en om een kiesbaar icoon. Dat zit er nu in. Er zijn **24
kleurthema's** in vier groepen (retroschermen zoals Game Boy, Virtual Boy, Commodore 64 en Terminal; thema's die prettig
zijn voor de ogen zoals Hoog contrast, Grijs, Nord en Solarized; kleurrijke thema's) en elk thema is op leesbaarheid
gemeten in een donkere en een lichte versie. Onder *Instellingen, Uiterlijk, Letters* kies je **duidelijke letters** in
plaats van pixelletters. Onder *Instellingen, App-icoon* kies je uit **acht iconen**. Na de update kan het icoon op je
beginscherm één keer verdwijnen; zet de app dan opnieuw vanuit het app-overzicht op je scherm. Niets is op een echte
telefoon bekeken, alleen in tests op de JVM. Meer in [docs/DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md).
