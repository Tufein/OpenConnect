## OpenConnect v0.30.0: calmer, cheaper syncs

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.30.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.29.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.30.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

- **A hiccup no longer ends a sync.** A dropped connection or a busy server (HTTP 429, 500, 502, 503, 504) is asked again after
  1.5 and then 5 seconds, for Garmin and for Oura. A `Retry-After` of up to 30 seconds is waited out; a longer one is not waited
  for in the middle of a sync, it only pauses the automatic syncs (below).
- **Automatic syncs pause after a rate limit**: for as long as the service asked, otherwise half an hour. This covers the sync when
  the app opens, the ten-minute timer and the background job. The Sync button still works.
- **A sync that finds nothing new now writes nothing.** Before, every Garmin sync rewrote the days it asked about and counted them as
  "updated", so every ten minutes the app reloaded its screens and (with the Health Connect switch on) sent all activities to Health
  Connect again. Now only days and nights whose numbers really changed are written and counted.
- **Your Oura login survives a busy Oura.** A refresh answered with 429, a server error or another unexpected status used to count
  as "login expired" and deleted the tokens, so you had to log in again. Only a refused refresh token (400 or 401) ends the login.
- **Health Connect gets only new or edited activities** after a sync. The *Send now* button still sends everything again, for when
  you emptied Health Connect. An edited activity (a new name, say) now replaces the old copy there, because it carries a rising
  `clientRecordVersion`.

### Not verified

- Nothing ran against the real Garmin, Oura or Health Connect services or on a phone. The waits (1.5 s, 5 s, 30 s cap, 30 minutes
  cool-down) are cautious guesses: Garmin publishes no rate limits. The documented Oura limit is 5,000 requests per 5 minutes, far
  above what the app does.
- `Retry-After` handling follows the HTTP standard; whether Garmin or Oura send it was not seen live.
- Health Connect's behaviour with `clientRecordVersion` follows Android's sync guide; it needs a phone with Health Connect to see.

### What changed in the code

- `core/Retry.kt` (new): `RetryPolicy` (`call` runs a request and asks again after the configured delays for an `IOException` or a
  transient status, `NONE` by default, `standard()` in the app) and `RetryAfter` (parses seconds or an HTTP date; `coolDown` gives the
  pause after a rate limit).
- `core/garmin`: `GarminResponse` and `OuraResponse` carry the answer's headers; `GarminApiException` and `OuraException` carry
  `retryAfterS`; `PersonalApiClient` and `OuraClient` take a `RetryPolicy`; `GarminSyncService.store` skips a day or night that comes
  out equal to what is stored; `OuraAuth.request` ends the login only for 400 or 401 and reports 429 as a rate limit.
- `core/health/ExportLedger.kt` (new) and `HealthConnectSync.export(onlyNew)`: the ledger of what was sent, fingerprint per activity,
  remembered batch by batch.
- App: `GarminController` and `OuraController` keep a cool-down after a rate limit and take the retry policy;
  `HealthConnectController.autoExport` sends only what is new; `SettingsStore.healthLedger` keeps the ledger;
  `AndroidHealthConnectGateway` writes a rising `clientRecordVersion`.
- Tests: `RetryTest`, new cases in `PersonalApiClientTest`, `GarminSyncTest`, `OuraTest`, `HealthConnectSyncTest`,
  `GarminBackgroundTest`, `HealthConnectControllerTest`, `SettingsStoreTest` and a new `OuraControllerTest`. `docs/SYNC.md` has the
  research behind it.

### Samenvatting (Nederlands)

Syncs zijn rustiger en zuiniger. Een korte haper of een drukke Garmin of Oura beëindigt een sync niet meer: er wordt na 1,5 en
5 seconden opnieuw gevraagd en een `Retry-After` wordt gerespecteerd. Na een rate limit wachten de automatische syncs (de knop
blijft werken). Een sync zonder nieuwe gegevens schrijft niets meer weg, zodat de app niet elke tien minuten alles herlaadt en
opnieuw naar Health Connect stuurt. Je Oura-login wordt niet meer weggegooid als Oura even druk is, en Health Connect krijgt alleen
nieuwe of gewijzigde activiteiten. Niets hiervan is tegen de echte diensten of op een telefoon geprobeerd.
