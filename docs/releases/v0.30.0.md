## OpenConnect v0.30.0: a look of its own

### Install

Download `openconnect-v0.30.0.apk` below and open it. It installs **over v0.29.0 and keeps your data and your keys**
(same signing key). Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Check the download against `openconnect-v0.30.0.apk.sha256`.

### What is new

The app looked like every other Material app with a dark theme. It now has one look of its own, "Handheld": the screen
of a handheld console.

- **Type.** *Jersey 10*, a pixel face made for sports shirts, for every headline and every big number; *IBM Plex Sans*
  for sentences and *IBM Plex Mono* for labels and units. The fonts are part of the app, so every phone draws the same
  letters.
- **Shapes.** Corners are cut in pixel steps. Panels are flat: a 2 dp outline in the text colour and a hard shadow, no
  gradients, no glow, no blur.
- **Colour.** Warm paper in light mode, warm black in dark mode, orange as the one signal colour. Each body signal
  (heart, Body Battery, stress, sleep, steps) keeps its own colour in every theme.
- **Icons and logo.** Pixel art, drawn by the app: the tab bar, the arrows, the logo (a pixel heart with a pulse
  through it) and the launcher icon, which is now the same heart on a dotted paper background.
- **Controls.** Buttons that sink onto their shadow when pressed, chips that fill with ink when chosen, a pixel
  switch, a four-square loader and block progress bars.
- **Charts.** Bar charts are stacks of blocks like a stereo level meter, line charts have a scan-line fill under a
  square-capped line, the step dial is 40 blocks around the number, and the readiness score on Home is a 25-block
  health bar next to a big number.
- **Tab bar.** A flat strip; the open tab is a block filled with ink, like the highlighted entry in a game menu. The
  labels shrink by themselves until the longest word fits, in any language and at any font size.
- **Home** has one layout now (the readiness score, the four signals, the step dial, goals and training load, the last
  days, the chart, the actions, the latest activities) instead of three.
- **Settings, Appearance** is shorter: the choice of design and style is gone, because there is only one. The colours
  are **Handheld** (default), **Game Boy**, **Amber terminal**, **Synthwave** and **Wallpaper** (the colours of your
  phone, Android 12 and newer). A theme saved by an older version (Arcade, Clean, Midnight, Ocean, Sunset) opens as
  Handheld.
- The first-start screen no longer asks for a design.
- The watch faces (Clean, Dashboard, Retro) are separate code for the watch and keep their own three designs.

The rules of the look, and how to add a screen in it, are in [docs/DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md).

### Not verified

- Nothing here ran on a phone. The screens were drawn and checked by UI tests on the JVM (the screenshots are in the
  CI run), not by hand on a real screen. Jersey is a small face and its sizes are guesses: if a size is off on your
  phone, that is what to tell me.
- The Wallpaper colours and the themed launcher icon were not looked at on a phone.
- This release does not change how syncing works.

### What changed in the code

- `ui/Theme.kt` (rewritten): the fonts, the five colour themes and the colours of the body signals, the typography
  (Jersey for display and titles), `PixelCornerShape` (a Material `CornerBasedShape` with stair-step corners, so every
  Material component that takes its shape from the theme gets them) and the theme itself. The `Design` enum is kept
  for the watch face previews only.
- `ui/Pixel.kt` (new): `Panel` (outline, hard shadow, tint), the three buttons, `PixelIconButton`, `PixelChip`,
  `PixelSwitch`, `PixelSpinner`, `PixelProgress` and `DottedRule`.
- `ui/PixelIcons.kt` (new): the icons and the logo as text grids and the function that draws them.
- `ui/Components.kt` and `ui/Charts.kt`: the shared pieces (cards, tiles, titles, dial, sparkline, charts) redone in
  the new look, without the three-way branching on design.
- Every screen: Material buttons, chips, switches, spinners and icons were replaced by the pixel ones (the `material-icons-core`
  dependency is gone); `Navigation.kt` has the new tab bar; `HomeScreen.kt` has one layout; `SettingsScreen.kt` and
  `OnboardingScreen.kt` lost the design and style choices.
- `AppViewModel.kt` and `SettingsStore.kt`: the look is just a colour theme now (default "handheld"); the saved design
  and style are no longer read. A small fix on the way: the state of the zones, calendar, shoes and plan is now created
  before the first load starts (it could still be empty when that load finished).
- Resources: four font files (Jersey 10 and IBM Plex, SIL Open Font License 1.1: texts in `licenses/`, credit in
  `NOTICE`), the new launcher icon, the widget on the home screen in the new colours, English and Dutch strings.
- Tests: `DesignsTest` is replaced by `LookTest` (an old theme opens as Handheld; the main screens in dark and in light;
  the first start); the theme test no longer checks styles; the app-lock switch carries a test tag.

### Samenvatting (Nederlands)

De app had het uiterlijk van elke andere donkere Material-app. Nu heeft hij één eigen uiterlijk, "Handheld": het
scherm van een handheld-console. Pixellettertype (Jersey 10 voor koppen en grote getallen), hoeken die in pixelstapjes
zijn afgesneden, vlakke panelen met een harde schaduw, pixeliconen en een pixelhart als logo (ook het
app-icoon), blokgrafieken en een blokbalk voor de paraatheid op Home. Warm papier in de lichte stand, warm zwart in de
donkere stand, oranje als enige signaalkleur; elk lichaamssignaal houdt zijn eigen kleur. De keuze uit drie
ontwerpen en drie stijlen is weg, want er is er nog maar één; de kleuren (Handheld, Game Boy, Amber terminal,
Synthwave, Achtergrond) kun je nog wel kiezen. Een oud thema opent als Handheld. De horlogewijzerplaten houden hun eigen
drie ontwerpen. Synchroniseren werkt zoals voorheen. Niets hiervan is op een echte telefoon bekeken; de schermen zijn
getekend en gecontroleerd met UI-tests op de JVM. Meer in [docs/DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md).
